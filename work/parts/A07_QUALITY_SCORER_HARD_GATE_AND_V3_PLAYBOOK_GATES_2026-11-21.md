### 3.2 `score()`: the arithmetic

- P05-S018 FACT (per step, L79 to L103): for each `name` in `self._step_names`, `weight: int = getattr(steps_cfg, name)` (L87) and `total_weights_possible += weight` (L88). If `name not in step_results`, one WARNING is logged ("quality_scorer.missing_step: step '%s' missing - excluded from scoring denominator", L92 to L95), the name is appended to `missing_steps` and `step_scores[name] = 0` (L96 to L97, comment "still report 0 in step_scores for transparency"). Otherwise `raw = float(step_results[name])` (L99), `weighted = raw * weight` (L100), `step_scores[name] = int(round(weighted))` (L101), `total_achieved += weighted` (L102) and `total_weights_present += weight` (L103). A key of `step_results` that is not one of the step names is ignored (the loop runs over the names only).
- P05-S019 FACT (the total, L105 to L120): if `total_weights_present > 0`, `final_score = (total_achieved / total_weights_present) * 100.0` (L107) and `total_score = min(100, int(round(final_score)))` (L108), then one INFO line "quality_scorer.proportional_scoring: effective_weights=%d / total_weights=%d (%.1f%%)" whose third value is `(total_weights_present / total_weights_possible) * 100.0` (L109 to L114); otherwise `total_score = 0` and one WARNING "quality_scorer.all_steps_missing: returning score 0 (no valid steps)" (L115 to L120). The total is capped at 100 and has no floor.
- P05-S020 INTERPRETATION and current external context (P05-S006): `round()` without `ndigits` rounds half to even and returns an integer, so at L108 a proportional value of 59.5 gives 60, 60.5 gives 60 and 64.5 gives 64, and at L101 a weighted step value of 2.5 gives 2 and 7.5 gives 8. The total is computed from the unrounded weighted values (L102, L107), so the sum of the rounded `step_scores` need not equal `total_score`. Units: each raw value is a step score (P04 section 7; nominally 0.0 to 1.0, QS3), each weight an integer number of points; the total is a percentage of the weights present.
- P05-S021 FACT (tier and `passed`, L122 to L134): `high_thr = self._weights.high_score_threshold`, `med_thr = self._weights.medium_score_threshold` (L122 to L123); `total_score >= high_thr` gives `"HIGH"` and increments `_fx_tier_high` (L124 to L128), else `total_score >= med_thr` gives `"MEDIUM"` (L129 to L130), else `"LOW"` (L131 to L132); `passed = total_score >= self._weights.min_pass_score` (L134). All three comparisons are inclusive. The result carries `min_pass_score=self._weights.min_pass_score` and `tier_thresholds={"high": high_thr, "medium": med_thr}` (L142 to L150).
- P05-S022 FACT (telemetry, L136 to L140): `_fx_score.inc()` on every call that reaches L138, and `_fx_ceiling.inc()` when `total_score > _G2_CEILING_TRIPWIRE`, a module constant 65 (L19) whose comment reads "the G2 achievable ceiling — 25/100 pts dead-at-0 + 20 pinned-at-half make >65 algebraically impossible today. A score above it means G2's dead inputs came alive" (L16 to L18); the HIGH-tier comment reads "unreachable today (IA-P2-03: threshold 80 > ceiling 65)" (L126 to L127). The counters change no result.

### 3.3 Failure behaviour and side effects

- P05-S023 FACT: the scorer has no `try`, no `except` and no `raise` statement (reading L1 to L150). Every attribute read of the configuration, every `float()` conversion, every `round()` and both divisions (L107, L113) are unguarded; an exception raised there leaves `score()`.
- P05-S024 INTERPRETATION (what can raise on the screener's route, and what follows): on the OFF and shadow paths `step_results` is the executor's dictionary, in which every value is a float that the executor stores (a step's return value, 0.0 for an ERROR step, 0.5 for a TIMEOUT step; P04-S034 to P04-S036), so `float()` at L99 does not raise; a NaN or infinite value would make `int(round(...))` raise at L101 or L108 (CPython's `PyLong_FromDouble`, P05-S006), and none of the step routes recorded by P04 (section 7) returns such a value from finite quote fields. ### 8.5 `gate_confirm` (L369 to L419)

- P05-S086 FACT: `gate_confirm(*, candle_open, candle_high, candle_low, candle_close, candle_volume, level, baseline_5m_volume, min_body_frac: float, volume_mult: float) -> GateVerdict` (the first seven `Optional[float]`). The values are converted with `float()` (L396 to L398); `rng = h - l`; `rng <= 0` gives FAIL "degenerate candle (high<=low)" (L399 to L401); `base <= 0` gives FAIL "no baseline volume → fail (must-have)" (L402 to L403). - P05-S087 INTERPRETATION: a pass needs the close strictly above the level, a body of at least `min_body_frac` of the candle's range (inclusive) and a volume of at least `volume_mult` times the baseline (inclusive). ### 8.6 `gate_pullback` (L422 to L466)

- P05-S088 FACT: `gate_pullback(*, level, session_low, lowest_5m_close, atr30, proximity_pct: float, proximity_atr_mult: float, hold_buffer_atr_mult: float) -> GateVerdict` (the first four `Optional[float]`). If any of `level`, `session_low`, `lowest_5m_close`, `atr30` is `None`: FAIL "missing data (level/session_low/lowest_close/ATR30) → fail (must-have)" (L444 to L446). After `float()` conversion (L447 to L448): `proximity = max(float(proximity_pct) * lvl, float(proximity_atr_mult) * a)` (L449); `hold_floor = lvl - float(hold_buffer_atr_mult) * a` (L450); `touched = slow <= lvl + proximity` (L451); `held = lclose >= hold_floor` (L452); the evidence holds the level, session low, lowest close, proximity and hold floor (four places) and the two booleans (L453 to L455). `not touched` gives FAIL "no touch: session_low ... > LEVEL+proximity ... - P05-S089 INTERPRETATION: "touched" is one-sided: any session low at or below `level + proximity` counts, including one far below the level, which then fails only if a five-minute close went below the hold floor; both tests are inclusive. Units: prices for the level, the lows and the ATR; `proximity_pct` multiplies the level directly (a fraction: 0.005 is 0.5 % of the level), and the two ATR multipliers are dimensionless. An `atr30` of 0 is not "missing": it gives `proximity = proximity_pct × level` and a hold floor equal to the level; a negative `atr30` puts the hold floor above the level. Unlike `gate_confirm`, NaN inputs fail here: a NaN level or session low makes `touched` false, and a NaN lowest close or ATR makes `held` false (the hold floor is NaN), because a pass needs both comparisons true (P05-S006). A NaN threshold behaves by its position (added in the check): a NaN `proximity_pct` makes `proximity` NaN and fails `touched`; a NaN `proximity_atr_mult` (or a NaN ATR) is dropped by `max()`, leaving `proximity = proximity_pct × level`; a NaN `hold_buffer_atr_mult` makes the floor NaN and fails `held` (current external context: the `max()` documentation states only that the first maximal item is returned; the local interpreter gives `max(1.0, nan)` = 1.0 and `max(nan, 1.0)` = nan). Like `gate_confirm` the gate is long-only by construction (support below, close above the floor).
