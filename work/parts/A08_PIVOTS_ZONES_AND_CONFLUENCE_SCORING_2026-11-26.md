## 3. The data model (`models.py`) and `Candle.from_kite`

### 3.1 `Candle` and `Candle.from_kite`

- P01-S014 FACT (`Candle`, L23 to L31): a `@dataclass(frozen=True)` with six fields in this order: `ts: datetime`, `open: float`, `high: float`, `low: float`, `close: float`, `volume: int`. Docstring L25: "One OHLCV bar. ts is timezone-aware (or naive IST) datetime." The class has no `__post_init__` and no validation; no field records the instrument, the interval, the source or whether the bar is complete. No unit is stated for the prices or the volume (INTERPRETATION: prices in the instrument's quote currency as delivered by the broker, volume as the broker's count for the interval; neither is converted).
- P01-S015 FACT (`Candle.from_kite`, L33 to L43): a `@staticmethod` taking `row: dict` (docstring L35: "Build from a kite.historical_data() dict row") and returning `Candle(ts=row["date"], open=float(row["open"]), high=float(row["high"]), low=float(row["low"]), close=float(row["close"]), volume=int(row.get("volume") or 0))`. It reads exactly six keys: `date`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close` by subscription and `volume` by `.get`. It reads no `oi` key and ignores every other key. It has no `try`; it logs nothing; it rounds nothing and reads no tick size.
- P01-S016 FACT (`ts`): `row["date"]` is stored unchanged: no type check, no parsing, no time-zone conversion, attachment or removal. INTERPRETATION (A05 RECONCILED TOPIC RECORD section 4.5, L129, and A05 AUDIT HA-15, which read the published `kiteconnect` 5.1.0 library): with that library the value is the `datetime` the library parsed from the server's timestamp, aware with the server's offset, or naive if the string carried none; the vendor's current example carries `+0530` (P01-S004 (a)). Whether the legacy server sent aware times throughout is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A05's records). The docstring's alternative "naive IST" is therefore a permitted form, not one that `from_kite` produces itself.
- P01-S017 INTERPRETATION (bad or missing price values; Python semantics, P01-S004 (b), P01-S005): a missing `date`, `open`, `high`, `low` or `close` key raises `KeyError`; a `None` price raises `TypeError`; a non-numeric string raises `ValueError`; a numeric string (for example `"101.5"`) is accepted and converted; a NaN or infinity (as a float or as a string such as `"nan"`) is accepted and stored. No relation between the values is checked: `high < low`, a close outside `[low, high]`, a zero or negative price all pass.
- P01-S018 INTERPRETATION (volume; Python semantics, P01-S005): `int(row.get("volume") or 0)` gives 0 for a missing key, `None`, `0`, `0.0`, an empty string or `False`; a fractional float is truncated toward zero; an integer string is converted; a string such as `"12.5"` raises `ValueError`; a NaN volume raises `ValueError` and an infinite one `OverflowError`; a negative volume passes unchanged. A consumer cannot tell a missing volume from a zero volume (A05 AUDIT HA-08 records the same, owner A08).
- P01-S019 FACT (where a conversion error goes; boundary, P3): `from_kite` raises to its caller. Its two non-test callers are `sr_detector/fetch.py` L126 (`candles = [Candle.from_kite(r) for r in (rows or [])]`), which is inside the `try` of `_fetch_one` (L121 to L136): any exception from any row makes the whole interval's result `None` with one WARNING "sr_detector fetch: historical_data failed for %s/%s: %s" (logic owned by the part that studies `fetch.py`); and `scripts/forward_shadow_record.py` L212 (A18's), inside `try ... except Exception: pass` (L211 to L215). INTERPRETATION: one malformed row discards the whole list for that symbol and interval; no row is skipped individually.
- P01-S020 FACT (other ways a `Candle` is built): `core/candle_math.py` L427 (A05's `resample`) builds `Candle(ts=bs, open=a["o"], high=a["h"], low=a["l"], close=a["c"], volume=a["v"])` directly, without `from_kite`; the tests build candles directly (for example `tests/unit/test_sr_detector_units.py` L24 to L27). ## 4. Swing pivots (`pivots.py`)

- P01-S038 FACT (signature, L24): `find_swing_pivots(candles: List[Candle], left: int, right: int) -> List[Pivot]`. Docstring L25 to L36: "candles: chronological OHLCV bars"; "left: bars required to the left of a pivot"; "right: bars required to the right of a pivot"; "Returns: Pivots in bar order (a bar may yield both a HIGH and a LOW pivot only in degenerate single-bar windows; normally one or neither)."
- P01-S039 FACT (guard, L37 to L40): `n = len(candles)`; it returns an empty list when `left < 1`, `right < 1` or `n < (left + right + 1)`. No other input is checked: not the types of `left` and `right`, not the candle values, not the order or uniqueness of the bars.
- P01-S040 FACT (the scan, L42 to L45): for each index `i` in `range(left, n - right)`, `window = candles[i - left : i + right + 1]` (the `left + right + 1` bars from `i - left` to `i + right`, the candidate included) and `others` is the window without the element at position `left` of the window (the test `(i - left + j) != i`, L45, excludes exactly the candidate's own position, by index, not by identity or value). INTERPRETATION: the first `left` bars and the last `right` bars are never examined, as SR-P2 states (L14 to L15: "The first `left` and last `right` bars can never be pivots (no full window) — they are skipped").
- P01-S041 FACT (swing HIGH, L47 to L49): a HIGH pivot is appended when `all(c.high >= w.high for w in window)` and `any(c.high > w.high for w in others)`, with `Pivot(price=c.high, ts=c.ts, kind="HIGH", bar_index=i)`. FACT (swing LOW, L51 to L53): the mirror, `all(c.low <= w.low for w in window)` and `any(c.low < w.low for w in others)`, appending `Pivot(price=c.low, ts=c.ts, kind="LOW", bar_index=i)`. The comparison is non-strict against the whole window and strict against at least one other bar, as SR-P1 states (L10 to L13: "high[i] >= every high in the window [i-left, i+right] AND strictly greater than at least one neighbour in the window (so a perfectly flat plateau does not mark every bar)"). Only `high` and `low` are read; `open`, `close`, `volume` and `ts` (except as the pivot's label) play no part.
- P01-S042 INTERPRETATION (ties and plateaus; from L48 and L52): a bar that equals the window's maximum high and has at least one strictly lower neighbour in its window is a HIGH pivot even when another bar in the same window has the same high. So a plateau of two or more equal highs with lower bars around it yields one HIGH pivot for every bar of the plateau whose window contains a lower bar, all at the same price; only a window in which every high is equal yields none. Example (left = right = 2): highs `[1, 2, 5, 5, 2, 1]` give HIGH pivots at indices 2 and 3, both at 5. The same holds for LOW pivots. SR-P1's parenthesis ("a perfectly flat plateau does not mark every bar") is met only for a perfectly flat window; the test `tests/unit/test_sr_detector_units.py` L43 to L45 states the expectation for a fully flat series (nine equal bars, no pivots) only.
- P01-S043 INTERPRETATION (one bar as both HIGH and LOW; from L48 and L52): a bar whose high is the window's maximum and whose low is the window's minimum, each strictly beyond at least one other bar (an outside bar that engulfs its window), yields both a HIGH and a LOW pivot, the HIGH first (L48 before L52). The docstring's "only in degenerate single-bar windows" (L34 to L35) does not describe this case, and a single-bar window cannot occur because `left` and `right` are at least 1 (L39).
- P01-S044 FACT (output order and content): pivots are appended in increasing `i`, HIGH before LOW at the same `i` (L42 to L53); `bar_index` is the position in the given list, not a time; `ts` is the candle's `ts` unchanged. The function returns a new list and does not modify `candles`.
- P01-S045 FACT and INTERPRETATION (input order): the function does not sort and does not read `ts` to order bars (L37 to L55); it treats list position as time. INTERPRETATION: (a) with `left == right` (every non-test caller passes the same `n` for both, section 8) the window is symmetric, so a list given in reverse chronological order yields the same set of pivot bars, with the same prices and `ts`, but in reversed output order and with `bar_index` counted from the newest bar; which bars are "the first `left`" and "the last `right`" then swaps, so the newest `left` bars and the oldest `right` bars are skipped. (b) A list in any other order (shuffled, or two series concatenated) yields pivots from positional neighbours that are not time neighbours, with no error. (c) A consumer that reads the output order as time (for example the most recent pivot as the last element) gets the oldest pivot from a reversed list (section 8, `regime/engine.py` L195 to L200).
- P01-S046 INTERPRETATION (duplicate and missing bars; from L44 to L53): a bar repeated consecutively (the same row twice) forms a two-bar plateau, so if it is a local extreme both copies become pivots at the same price and `ts` (P01-S042), and the zone built from them counts two touches (section 5). A missing bar (a gap in the series) is not detected: the window counts list elements, not time, so the window spans more time than `left + right + 1` intervals.
- P01-S047 INTERPRETATION (numeric edge cases; Python semantics, P01-S005): a NaN `high` makes `c.high >= w.high` false for its own bar and for every bar whose window contains it (any comparison with NaN is false), so no HIGH pivot is found in any window that contains a NaN high; the same holds for NaN lows. An infinite high can be a HIGH pivot with price `inf`. Zero or negative prices are compared like any other numbers. No exception is raised for these values. A non-integer `left` or `right` would raise `TypeError` in `range` or in the slice; every non-test caller passes an `int` (section 8).
- P01-S048 INTERPRETATION (lag of the most recent structure; from L42 and SR-P2): because the last `right` bars are never examined, the most recent bar that can be a pivot is `right` bars before the last bar given. With the committed `default_pivot_n: 5` (`config/system_config.yaml` L513; `pivot_n_by_tf` not set in the file, loader default empty, `core/config_loader.py` L1169) this is five bars: about a trading week on the daily series, and five 60-minute or five 30-minute bars on the intraday series, which can reach back across the overnight break (committed value only; the deployed value is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED, Document 06, section 4.2). If the last bar given is still forming, it can serve as a right-hand neighbour but cannot itself be a pivot; whether the broker's answers included a forming last candle is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A05 AUDIT HA-04).
- P01-S049 FACT (cost, failure, side effects): the function makes `n - left - right` window slices of `left + right + 1` bars each; it catches no exception, logs nothing and reads no clock or configuration.

## 5. Zone clustering and volume-profile nodes (`zones.py`)

### 5.1 `cluster_zones`

- P01-S050 FACT (signature, L28 to L42): `cluster_zones(pivots: List[Pivot], *, cluster_pct: float, timeframe: str) -> List[Zone]`; `cluster_pct` and `timeframe` are keyword-only and have no default. Docstring: "pivots from one timeframe (mixed HIGH/LOW ok)"; "cluster_pct: a pivot joins the running cluster while its price is within this percent of the cluster's current band-high (e.g. 0.5 = 0.5%)"; "timeframe: label stamped on each produced Zone."
- P01-S051 FACT (per kind, L44 to L49): the loop takes `("HIGH", "RESISTANCE")` first and `("LOW", "SUPPORT")` second; for each it selects the pivots whose `kind` equals the pivot kind and sorts them by `price` ascending (`sorted(..., key=lambda p: p.price)`, L45 to L47); if there are none, the kind is skipped. A pivot whose `kind` is neither `"HIGH"` nor `"LOW"` is ignored without a log. INTERPRETATION (P01-S004 (b)): the sort is stable, so pivots of equal price keep their input order; that order does not affect the band, the touch count or the latest touch.
- P01-S052 FACT (the greedy pass, L51 to L60): the running cluster starts as the lowest pivot; for each following pivot `p`, `band_high = max(x.price for x in cluster)`, `tol = band_high * (cluster_pct / 100.0)`, and `p` joins the cluster if `p.price <= band_high + tol`; otherwise the cluster is closed into a zone (`_zone_from_cluster`) and a new cluster starts with `p`. After the loop the last cluster is closed (L60). Output: all RESISTANCE zones in ascending price, then all SUPPORT zones in ascending price.
- P01-S053 INTERPRETATION (the tolerance and chaining; from L52 to L56): the tolerance is a percentage of price (not an absolute amount and not ATR-based). Because the pivots are sorted ascending, `band_high` is always the price of the previous pivot, so the rule is: a pivot joins when its distance above the previous pivot is at most `cluster_pct` per cent of the previous pivot's price. Membership is therefore chained (single linkage): a zone can grow without limit while each consecutive step is within the tolerance. Example (`cluster_pct` 0.5): pivots at 100.0, 100.4, 100.8, 101.2, 101.6 and 102.0 (each step 0.4, below the tolerance of about 0.5) form one zone from 100.0 to 102.0 (2 per cent wide), although its ends are about four tolerances apart. No maximum band width exists. This is what SR-Z1 (L11 to L13) states: "a pivot joins the running cluster while it is within `cluster_pct` of the cluster's current high". The test `tests/unit/test_sr_detector_units.py` L55 to L67 states the expectation for 100.0, 100.2, 100.4 (one zone, three touches) and 105.0 (a separate zone).
- P01-S054 INTERPRETATION (minimum touches and zero-width zones; from L51 to L60): there is no minimum number of touches: a lone pivot becomes a zone with `touches = 1` and `band_low == band_high`, a band of zero width. The `Zone` docstring's "A price BAND (never a single line)" (`models.py` L62) is not enforced by the code: a zone can be a single price, and so can a cluster of pivots at one price. Downstream, a buffer applied around such a band (for example `contains` with a positive `buffer_pct`) gives it a width; the four files do not add one themselves.
- P01-S055 INTERPRETATION (parameter edge cases; from L54 to L55): `cluster_pct = 0` joins only pivots of exactly equal price; a negative `cluster_pct` makes even equal positive prices form separate zones; neither is rejected (the function checks nothing; `SRDetectorConfig.cluster_pct` is a plain `float`, `core/config_loader.py` L1171, with no validator in L1225 to L1250). The committed value is `cluster_pct: 0.5` (`config/system_config.yaml` L514), equal to the loader default (L1171) and to the `build_zone_knobs` fallback (`sr_detector/zone_builder.py` L45); the deployed value is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (Document 06, section 4.2). NaN pivot prices cannot come from `find_swing_pivots` (P01-S047); an infinite pivot price would form a zone of its own at the top of the list.
- P01-S056 INTERPRETATION (what a touch is; from L72 and P01-S042, P01-S046): `touches` counts pivots, not distinct swings, bars or days. A plateau of equal extremes (P01-S042) or a duplicated bar (P01-S046) yields several pivots at one price, all in one zone, so the touch count can exceed the number of separate swings. A bar that is both a HIGH and a LOW pivot (P01-S043) adds one touch to a RESISTANCE zone and one to a SUPPORT zone.

### 5.2 `_zone_from_cluster`

- P01-S057 FACT (L65 to L75): `prices = [p.price for p in cluster]`; `last_ts = max((p.ts for p in cluster), default=None)`; it returns `Zone(band_low=min(prices), band_high=max(prices), kind=kind, touches=len(cluster), timeframe=timeframe, last_touch_ts=last_ts)`. This matches SR-Z2 (L14 to L15): "Zone band = [min, max] of the cluster's pivot prices. touches = count of pivots; last_touch_ts = most recent pivot ts (recency)."
- P01-S058 INTERPRETATION (time fields; from L67 and P01-S004 (e)): the only time kept is the latest pivot `ts`; the earliest touch, the list of touch times and the bar indices are not kept, and the `Zone` does not keep its pivots. The cluster is never empty when this helper is called (L51, L59), so `default=None` is not reached from `cluster_zones`. `max` over the `ts` values uses order comparisons, so a cluster that mixes naive and aware `datetime` values, or holds a `None` `ts` beside a `datetime`, raises `TypeError` (a single-pivot cluster returns its one value without a comparison). Within one fetched series the `ts` values come from one parser (P01-S016), so a mix is not expected from the main path (INTERPRETATION). `ts` is the candle's label; A05's records read the broker's label as the start of the interval (A05 RECONCILED TOPIC RECORD section 4.5, L130), so the latest touch is dated at the start of the bar that made the extreme, not at the moment the extreme printed.

### 5.3 `volume_profile_nodes`

- P01-S059 FACT (signature, L78 to L90): `volume_profile_nodes(candles: List[Candle], *, bins: int = 24, node_frac: float = 0.7) -> List[float]`. Docstring: "Return the centre prices of high-volume price bins (volume nodes, SR-Z4). A crude volume-by-price: bucket each bar's volume at its mid price across `bins` equal-width buckets over [min low, max high]; a node is any bucket whose total volume >= node_frac * (max bucket volume)."
- P01-S060 FACT (guards, L91 to L96): it returns an empty list when `candles` is empty or `bins < 1`; it computes `lo = min(c.low for c in candles)` and `hi = max(c.high for c in candles)` and returns an empty list when `hi <= lo`.
- P01-S061 FACT (binning, L98 to L107): `width = (hi - lo) / bins`; `buckets = [0.0] * bins`; for each candle, `mid = (c.high + c.low) / 2.0`, `idx = int((mid - lo) / width)`, set to `bins - 1` if `idx >= bins` and to 0 if `idx < 0`, then `buckets[idx] += float(c.volume)`.
- P01-S062 INTERPRETATION (what the binning means; from L98 to L107): the buckets are equal in price width, `(max high − min low) / bins`, so their width scales with the range of the window, not with the price level. Each bar's whole volume goes to the one bucket that contains its mid-range price `(high + low) / 2`; it is not spread over the bar's range and does not use the close or a typical price. `int()` truncates toward zero (P01-S004 (b)), so `idx` is the bucket whose lower edge is at or below `mid`. For valid bars `mid` lies within `[lo, hi]`, so the clamps (L103 to L106) act only when `mid == hi` (a zero-range bar at the window's top, `idx == bins`) or for an invalid bar with `high < low` whose mid falls outside the range.
- P01-S063 FACT (nodes, L109 to L116): `peak = max(buckets)`; if `peak <= 0` it returns an empty list; otherwise it returns `lo + (i + 0.5) * width` for every bucket `i` with `v >= node_frac * peak`, in ascending price order. INTERPRETATION: the nodes are bucket centres, not traded prices; adjacent node buckets are not merged; the number of nodes is not limited; a series with zero volume everywhere (for example volumes defaulted to 0 by `from_kite`, P01-S018) gives no nodes.
- P01-S064 INTERPRETATION (parameter and value edge cases; from L91 to L116, P01-S005): `node_frac <= 0` makes every bucket a node, empty ones included (`v >= 0` holds), and `node_frac > 1` makes none; neither is rejected (`volume_node_frac` is a plain `float`, `core/config_loader.py` L1176). A non-integer `bins` raises `TypeError` at `[0.0] * bins`. A negative volume reduces its bucket. A NaN or infinite high or low in any candle leads to `int()` of a NaN at L102 (directly, or through a NaN or infinite `width`), which raises `ValueError` (P01-S005); the function does not catch it. A NaN volume makes its bucket NaN, which never satisfies `v >= node_frac * peak`; whether it becomes `peak` depends on its position, because `max` compares with `<`/`>` and comparisons with NaN are false.
- P01-S065 FACT (committed values and fallbacks): `config/system_config.yaml` L508 to L545 has no `volume_bins` or `volume_node_frac` key, so the loader defaults apply (`volume_bins: int = 24`, `volume_node_frac: float = 0.7`, `core/config_loader.py` L1175 to L1176), equal to the `build_zone_knobs` fallbacks (`sr_detector/zone_builder.py` L46 to L47) and the function defaults (L81 to L82). The deployed values are UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED.
- P01-S066 FACT (which candles are profiled; interface only): the one non-test caller, `sr_detector/zone_builder.py` L99 to L103, passes `daily = tf_candles.get("day") or next(iter(tf_candles.values()))` with `bins=knobs.volume_bins` and `node_frac=knobs.volume_node_frac`. INTERPRETATION: the profile is built from the daily bars of the fetch window when a non-empty `"day"` series is present (committed `lookback_days: 180`, `config/system_config.yaml` L511), and otherwise from the first series in the dict; the choice is the zone builder's logic, owned by the part that studies `zone_builder.py`.

- P01-S070 FACT (`score_zones`, L62 to L73): signature `score_zones(zones_by_tf: Dict[str, List[Zone]], ctx: ConfluenceContext, params: ScoringParams) -> List[ScoredZone]`; docstring "Merge across TFs and score each merged zone. For `kind` in `("RESISTANCE", "SUPPORT")` it calls `_merge_across_tfs(zones_by_tf, kind, params.merge_pct)` and appends `_score_group(group, kind, ctx, params)` for every group. Output order: all RESISTANCE groups, then all SUPPORT groups, each in the order the merge produced (ascending centre, P01-S072). Nothing is filtered out: every group is returned, LOW ones included, and nothing is sorted by score. An empty `zones_by_tf` gives an empty list. A `Zone` whose `kind` is neither value is dropped without a log (L86).
- P01-S071 FACT (`_merge_across_tfs`, L80 to L101): it flattens the zones of the given kind from every timeframe in the iteration order of `zones_by_tf.values()` (L84 to L86), sorts them by `center` ascending (`flat.sort(key=lambda z: z.center)`, L87), and builds groups greedily: the first zone starts a group; each next zone `z` is compared with the current (last) group's mean centre `cur_center = sum(x.center for x in cur) / len(cur)` (L95), with `tol = cur_center * (merge_pct / 100.0)` (L96); if `abs(z.center - cur_center) <= tol` it joins the group, otherwise it starts a new one (L97 to L100). Docstring: "Greedy-cluster same-kind zones from all TFs by centre proximity."
- P01-S072 INTERPRETATION (the merge rule; from L84 to L100 and P01-S004 (b), (c)): (a) the tolerance is a percentage of the group's running mean centre, unweighted by touches or width, and is compared with the distance between centres, not between band edges, so two overlapping bands with distant centres can stay apart and two disjoint bands with close centres can merge; (b) because the zones are sorted ascending, the mean drifts upward as members join, so a group can extend beyond `merge_pct` of its lowest member (a milder form of the chaining of P01-S053); (c) the rule does not require the members to come from different timeframes: two zones of the same timeframe join one group when the second falls within the tolerance of the group's mean, which can happen through a zone of another timeframe between them. Example with the committed values (`cluster_pct` 0.5, `merge_pct` 0.4): daily single-pivot zones at 100.0 and 100.55 stay separate in `cluster_zones` (100.55 is above 100.0 + 0.5); a 60-minute zone at 100.35 joins the 100.0 zone (distance 0.35, tolerance 0.4), the group mean becomes 100.175, and the 100.55 zone joins too (distance 0.375, tolerance about 0.4007), giving one group with two daily zones and one 60-minute zone, `timeframes` of length 2; (d) the sort is stable and `dict` iteration follows insertion order, so zones with equal centres keep the timeframe order of `zones_by_tf` (on the configured path, the order of the fetched series); with equal centres this order does not change the groups.
- P01-S073 INTERPRETATION (parameter edge cases): `merge_pct = 0` merges only zones with exactly equal centres; a negative `merge_pct` or a non-positive mean centre makes the tolerance negative, so nothing merges (an `abs` value is never below a negative number); NaN centres cannot arise from zones built by `cluster_zones` from `find_swing_pivots` output (P01-S047, P01-S055).
- P01-S074 FACT and INTERPRETATION (SR-C1 against the code): SR-C1 (L11 to L12) reads "Merge same-kind zones ACROSS timeframes by centre proximity (within merge_pct) into one band; the merged band spans the union." FACT: the merged band is the union `[min band_low, max band_high]` (`_score_group` L107 to L108), so a gap between members is inside the merged band. INTERPRETATION: "ACROSS timeframes" describes the purpose; the code also merges zones of one timeframe (P01-S072 (c)); "within merge_pct" is measured from the group's mean centre, not pairwise.

### 6.3 `_score_group` and the scoring formula

- P01-S075 FACT (group summary, L107 to L114): `band_low = min(z.band_low for z in group)`; `band_high = max(z.band_high for z in group)`; `timeframes = tuple(sorted({z.timeframe for z in group}))` (distinct labels, sorted as strings, so `"30minute" < "60minute" < "day"`); `total_touches = sum(z.touches for z in group)`; `last_touch = max((z.last_touch_ts for z in group if z.last_touch_ts is not None), default=None)`.
- P01-S076 FACT (the votes, L116 to L143), appended to `evidence` in this fixed order:
  1. `swing = params.w_swing * min(total_touches, params.touch_cap)`, appended as `("swing_pivots", swing)` only if `swing > 0` (L119 to L121);
  2. `multi = params.w_multi_tf * len(timeframes)`, appended as `("multi_tf", multi)` only if `multi > 0` (L124 to L126);
  3. `("volume_node", params.w_volume)` if `_level_in_band(ctx.volume_nodes, band_low, band_high, params.band_buffer_pct)` (L129 to L130);
  4. `("prior_day_level", params.w_prior_day)` if `_level_in_band(ctx.prior_day_levels, band_low, band_high, params.band_buffer_pct)` (L133 to L134);
  5. `("round_number", params.w_round)` if `_has_round_number(band_low, band_high)` (L137 to L138);
  6. `rfac = _recency_factor(last_touch, ctx, params)`; `("recency", params.w_recency * rfac)` only if `rfac > 0 and params.w_recency > 0` (L141 to L143).
  Then `score = sum(c for _, c in evidence)` (L145), `distinct_types = len(evidence)` (L146) and `confidence = _bucket(score, distinct_types, len(timeframes), params)` (L147). The `ScoredZone` carries the union band, `kind`, `score`, `confidence`, `touches=total_touches` (uncapped), `timeframes`, `evidence=tuple(evidence)` and `last_touch_ts=last_touch` (L149 to L159).
- P01-S077 FACT and INTERPRETATION (formula): the score is

  `score = w_swing·min(T, touch_cap) + w_multi_tf·N_tf + w_volume·[volume node in band ± buffer] + w_prior_day·[PDH, PDL or PDC in band ± buffer] + w_round·[round number in band] + w_recency·r`

  where `T` is the summed touches of the group, `N_tf` the number of distinct timeframe labels, `[...]` is 1 when the test holds and 0 otherwise, and `r` the recency factor of section 6.5. Each external vote counts once, however many levels or nodes fall in the band. INTERPRETATION (arithmetic on the committed values, P01-S069): with three timeframes the score lies between 2 (one touch, one timeframe, no other vote) and 10 (4 + 3 + 1 + 1 + 0.5 + 0.5).
- P01-S078 INTERPRETATION ("distinct method-types"; from L119 to L126 and L146): for any group (each zone has at least one touch, P01-S054) with `w_swing > 0` and `w_multi_tf > 0`, both `swing_pivots` and `multi_tf` are always appended, so `distinct_types` is always at least 2. With the committed weights the condition "≥2 distinct method-types" of SR-C3 is therefore always met, and the HIGH test reduces to `score >= t_high` and at least two timeframes. `multi_tf` is appended for a single-timeframe group too (`1.0 × 1`), so it counts as a method even when no second timeframe agrees.
- P01-S079 INTERPRETATION (what reaches each bucket with the committed values; from P01-S076 to P01-S078, P01-S080): (a) every group that has zones from all three configured timeframes has `swing >= 3` and `multi = 3`, so `score >= 6` and it is HIGH without any volume, prior-day, round-number or recency vote; (b) a two-timeframe group is HIGH when its summed touches are at least 3 (swing ≥ 3 + multi 2 = 5), or with two touches plus one volume or prior-day vote, or with two touches plus the round-number vote and a full recency factor of 1.0; (c) a single-timeframe group can never be HIGH, whatever its score (up to 8); it is MEDIUM from two touches (2 + 1 = 3), or from one touch plus one volume or prior-day vote; (d) a single pivot on one timeframe with no other vote scores 2 (LOW), or 2.5 with a round number or up to 2.5 with recency, reaching MEDIUM only with both and `r = 1.0`. The test `tests/unit/test_sr_detector_units.py` L83 to L95 states the expectation that two zones with five and three touches on two timeframes, with no external evidence, are HIGH with `swing_pivots` and `multi_tf` as their evidence; L104 to L108 that four touches on one timeframe are MEDIUM, "only 1 TF -> cannot be HIGH"; L98 to L101 that one touch on one timeframe is LOW.
- P01-S080 FACT (`_bucket`, L162 to L167): returns `"HIGH"` if `score >= p.t_high and distinct_types >= 2 and num_tfs >= 2`; else `"MEDIUM"` if `score >= p.t_med`; else `"LOW"`. Both thresholds are inclusive. Nothing checks that `t_high >= t_med`.
- P01-S081 INTERPRETATION (zero or negative weights; from L120, L125, L129 to L138, L142): the swing, multi-timeframe and recency votes are appended only when positive, but the volume, prior-day and round-number votes are appended whenever their test holds, whatever their weight. A weight of 0 for one of these three would still add an evidence entry (contribution 0) and count it in `distinct_types`; a negative weight would lower the score. The committed weights are all positive (P01-S069), so this is latent on the committed path.
- P01-S082 INTERPRETATION (floating point and rounding): the score is a sum of floats and is compared with the thresholds without rounding; the recency contribution is a continuous value, so a score can sit just below a threshold by a fraction. `ScoredZone.to_dict` rounds the score to four decimals for storage (P01-S028); the bucket is decided on the unrounded value.

### 6.4 `_level_in_band` and `_has_round_number`

- P01-S083 FACT (`_level_in_band`, L170 to L177): returns `False` for an empty `levels`; otherwise `center = (low + high) / 2.0`, `pad = center * (buffer_pct / 100.0)`, and returns `any((low - pad) <= lv <= (high + pad) for lv in levels)`. INTERPRETATION: the same inclusive, centre-relative percentage buffer as `Zone.contains` (P01-S024), applied to the merged union band; with the committed `band_buffer_pct: 0.1` the band is widened by 0.1 per cent of its centre on each side. A NaN level never matches.
- P01-S084 FACT (`_has_round_number`, L180 to L192): docstring "A multiple of the price magnitude (or half-magnitude) falling in the band." It computes `center = (low + high) / 2.0`; returns `False` if `center <= 0`; sets `magnitude = 10.0 ** math.floor(math.log10(center))` (comment "e.g. 523 -> 100"); for `step` in `(magnitude, magnitude / 2.0)` (skipping a non-positive step) it computes `k = math.ceil(low / step - 1e-9)` and returns `True` if `k * step <= high + 1e-9`; otherwise `False`. No buffer is applied.
- P01-S085 INTERPRETATION (the round-number rule; from L185 to L191): (a) `k * step` is the smallest multiple of `step` at or above `low` (the `- 1e-9` lets a `low` a hair above a multiple, in units of the step, count as that multiple), and the test asks whether it lies at or below `high` (with an absolute `1e-9` allowance): that is, whether the band contains a multiple of the step. (b) Every multiple of `magnitude` is also a multiple of `magnitude / 2`, so the first step adds nothing: the rule is in effect "the band contains a multiple of half the power of ten below its centre". (c) The step depends on the decade of the centre: for a centre from 100 to just under 1,000 the step is 50; from 1,000 to just under 10,000 it is 500; from 10 to just under 100 it is 5. Relative to price the step therefore ranges from 50 per cent (centre at a power of ten) down to just over 5 per cent (centre just under the next power of ten), and it jumps tenfold when the centre crosses a power of ten (a band centred at 99.9 tests multiples of 5; one centred at 100.1 tests multiples of 50). (d) A zero-width band (`low == high`, P01-S054) matches only when its single price is a multiple of the step within the tolerances. (e) Example: a band 498 to 502 (centre 500, step 50) matches at 500; a band 520 to 521 (centre 520.5) does not, the next multiple of 50 above 520 being 550.
- P01-S086 INTERPRETATION (edge cases; P01-S005): a non-positive centre returns `False`; a NaN centre passes the `center <= 0` test (it is false), `math.log10` returns NaN and `math.floor` of NaN raises `ValueError`; an infinite centre gives `math.log10(inf) = inf` and `math.floor(inf)` raises `OverflowError`. Neither is caught in the function or in `_score_group`. NaN or infinite bands cannot come from pivots produced by `find_swing_pivots` except through an infinite pivot price (P01-S047).
- P01-S087 FACT (a second round-number rule in the package, cross-reference only): `sr_detector/zone_builder.py` L124 to L140 (`round_number_levels`, the zone-builder part's) uses the same magnitude and half-magnitude steps to list the round levels just below and above a price (rounded to six decimals), for the anchor payload. It is not called by the four files; `_has_round_number` does not use it.

### 6.5 `_recency_factor`: the time base

- P01-S088 FACT (`_recency_factor`, L195 to L211): returns `0.0` if `last_touch is None`; sets `now = ctx.now` and returns `1.0` if `now is None`; if exactly one of `now` and `last_touch` is naive (`(now.tzinfo is None) != (last_touch.tzinfo is None)`), it replaces the `tzinfo` of both with `None` (comment L203: "tz-safety: kite candle ts may be aware (IST) while now_fn() differs."); computes `age_days = (now - last_touch).total_seconds() / 86400.0`; returns `1.0` if `ctx.recency_window_days <= 0`; otherwise `factor = 1.0 - (age_days / ctx.recency_window_days)` and returns `max(p.recency_min_factor, min(1.0, factor))`.
- P01-S089 INTERPRETATION (what the factor measures; from L207 to L211 and P01-S004 (e)): (a) the decay is linear in calendar time (seconds divided by 86,400), counting weekends, holidays and overnight hours, not trading days or bars: a touch `w` days old gets `1 − w/90` with the default window, reaching 0 at 90 days; (b) the factor is clamped to at most 1.0, so a `last_touch` later than `now` (for example a bar label in the future relative to an `as_of` clock) gets the full factor; (c) the lower clamp is `recency_min_factor` (0.0 committed through the loader default), so a touch older than the window gives 0.0 and no `recency` entry (L142); a `recency_min_factor` above 0 would give every dated zone a recency vote, and one above 1 would return that value, above the upper clamp (no validation, P01-S069); (d) `last_touch` is the latest pivot's bar label (P01-S058), and pivots are never closer to the end of the series than `right` bars (P01-S048), so the most recent possible touch is several bars old; with the committed values a daily touch seven calendar days old gives `r ≈ 0.922` and a contribution of about 0.461.
- P01-S090 INTERPRETATION (time-zone handling; from L203 to L207 and P01-S004 (e)): when one side is aware and the other naive, both are reduced to their wall-clock digits; the age is right only if the aware value's wall clock is in the same zone as the naive value's (both IST, as the `Candle` docstring and the comment assume). When both are aware with different `tzinfo`, Python subtracts them as UTC instants, which is correct. When both are naive, the age is the wall-clock difference. No case raises.
- P01-S091 INTERPRETATION (full credit when no clock is given; from L200 to L202 and L208 to L209): a `None` `now` or a non-positive window gives every dated zone `r = 1.0`, the maximum recency vote, rather than none. The function does not read a clock itself (P01-S010); the time base is whatever the caller places in `ConfluenceContext.now` (section 8: the detector's and warmer's `_safe_now()`, which return `None` if the clock function raises, `sr_detector/detector.py` L305 to L309, `sr_detector/zone_warmer.py` L146 to L150, `v3_chain/watchlist_capture.py` L232 to L236; the V3 runners' `as_of`).

### 6.6 Ordering, ties and determinism

- P01-S092 INTERPRETATION (determinism; from P01-S010 and P01-S004 (b), (c)): the four files are deterministic functions of their inputs: no clock, no randomness, no global state; sorts are stable and `dict` iteration follows insertion order. The same candles, parameters and `now` give the same zones, scores, buckets and output order. The output depends on input order only through the pivot positions (P01-S045) and through the timeframe order of `zones_by_tf` for zones with equal centres (P01-S072 (d)).
- P01-S093 FACT (output order and its consumers, interface only): `score_zones` returns zones by kind and ascending centre, not by score or by distance to any price (P01-S070). `sr_detector/detector.py` L253 to L254 logs `[z.to_dict() for z in scored if z.kind == ...][: self._max_zones_logged]` for each kind (`max_zones_logged` not in the YAML; loader default 12, `core/config_loader.py` L1197). INTERPRETATION: when there are more than that many zones of one kind, the ones kept in the log are the lowest-priced, not the strongest or the nearest; the slicing is the detector's logic and is recorded here only because it depends on this order (owned by the part that studies `detector.py`). Consumers that choose a zone by price select by their own key (`min`/`max` on centre or band edge: `sr_detector/flags.py` L122, L133, L135; `screening/retest_monitor.py` L390; `orders/structure_exit_manager.py` L270, L277; `v3_chain/watchlist_capture.py` L214), so they do not depend on the order except for ties, where `min`/`max` return the first item encountered (P01-S004 (b)).

## 7. Input-order, data-quality and numeric edge cases across the four files (summary)

This section gathers the cases already stated, so that part P07 and the assembly can cite one place for A05's handoffs (A05 AUDIT CM-05, CM-10, HA-04, HA-08, HA-15; the A08 row L1677).

- P01-S094 INTERPRETATION (input order; from P01-S021, P01-S045, P01-S051, P01-S058, P01-S061, P01-S071): nothing in the chain from `Candle.from_kite` to `score_zones` orders candles by time. Only `find_swing_pivots` depends on candle order, and it uses list position as time. `cluster_zones` and `_merge_across_tfs` re-sort by price and by centre, `_zone_from_cluster` and `_score_group` take the latest `ts` with `max`, and `volume_profile_nodes` sums per bucket, so none of these depends on order. Consequences: (a) a series in descending time gives the same zones and scores as the same series ascending, because every caller uses equal `left` and `right` (P01-S045 (a)); (b) a series in any other order gives pivots from non-neighbouring bars and therefore different zones, with no error; (c) a consumer that reads the pivot list's order as time is affected even by a descending series (P01-S045 (c); `regime/engine.py` L195 to L200, A18's). Outside the four files, `prior_day_levels` (`sr_detector/zone_builder.py` L78 to L82) takes `daily[-2]` as the prior day, which assumes ascending order (the zone-builder part's logic). Whether the server's rows ever arrived out of order is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A05 AUDIT CM-05).
- P01-S095 INTERPRETATION (duplicates and gaps; from P01-S046, P01-S056, P01-S061): a duplicated bar can create two pivots at one price and time and so two touches, which raises the swing vote (up to `touch_cap`) and can lift a zone's bucket (P01-S079); its volume is counted twice in its price bucket. A gap (missing bars) is not detected anywhere in the four files; a pivot window then spans more time than its bar count suggests. No function de-duplicates by `ts`.
- P01-S096 INTERPRETATION (a still-forming last bar; from P01-S048, P01-S062): a forming last bar cannot be a pivot (it is among the last `right` bars) but its high and low can decide whether the bars before it are pivots, and its partial volume enters the volume profile at its current mid. Whether the broker's answers included such a bar is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A05 AUDIT HA-04).
- P01-S097 FACT and INTERPRETATION (bad values, by function): `from_kite` raises on a missing key, a `None` price or a non-numeric string, accepts NaN and infinity, and turns a missing or `None` volume into 0 (P01-S017, P01-S018); `find_swing_pivots` silently finds no pivot in any window containing a NaN and can emit an infinite pivot (P01-S047); `cluster_zones` and `_merge_across_tfs` raise nothing for finite or infinite prices (P01-S055, P01-S073); `volume_profile_nodes` raises `ValueError` for any NaN or infinite high or low (P01-S064); `_has_round_number` raises `ValueError` or `OverflowError` for a NaN or infinite band centre (P01-S086); a mix of naive and aware `ts` values raises `TypeError` in `_zone_from_cluster` or `_score_group` (P01-S058, P01-S075) but not in `_recency_factor` (P01-S090). None of the four files checks `high >= low`, positive prices or non-negative volume, and none catches an exception. Where an exception goes is the callers' logic (section 8). Whether any such value reached the code in production is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A05 AUDIT CM-10).
- P01-S098 INTERPRETATION (zero and negative prices; from P01-S024, P01-S055, P01-S073, P01-S084): no function rejects a zero or negative price. The percentage tolerances and buffers are multiples of a price, so they shrink to zero or turn negative for such prices: `contains` and `_level_in_band` narrow the band, `cluster_zones` stops joining equal prices, `_merge_across_tfs` merges nothing, and `_has_round_number` returns `False`.
- P01-S099 INTERPRETATION (zero volume; from P01-S018, P01-S063): a series whose volumes are all 0 (or defaulted to 0 by `from_kite`) gives no volume nodes, so no zone gets the volume vote; nothing else in the four files reads volume. Where only some volumes are 0, the profile is computed from the others without any sign that values were missing (A05 AUDIT HA-08).
- P01-S100 INTERPRETATION (short series and single candles; from P01-S039, P01-S060 to P01-S063): with `left = right = 5` a series needs at least 11 bars before any pivot can be found; a shorter series yields no pivots and no zones for that timeframe. A single candle with `high > low` gives exactly one volume node (the centre of the bucket at the middle of its own range); a single zero-range candle gives none (`hi <= lo`).
- P01-S101 FACT and INTERPRETATION (units, across the four files): every tolerance is a percentage of a price and none uses ATR, a tick size or an absolute amount: `cluster_pct` is relative to the previous pivot's price (P01-S053), `merge_pct` to the group's mean centre (P01-S072), `band_buffer_pct` and `buffer_pct` to the band's centre (P01-S024, P01-S083). The pivot window counts bars (P01-S040); the recency window counts calendar days (P01-S089); the volume bins are equal fractions of the window's price range (P01-S062). No value is rounded to a tick in the four files; only `to_dict` rounds, to four decimals (P01-S028).

## 10. Points for the AUDIT

Neutral points for the A08 AUDIT, each citing the statements it rests on. "Boundary" marks a point whose code or record another topic or part owns; it is reported, not resolved. No verdict is given.

- P01-AP01 — What makes a zone HIGH: SR-C3, the configuration comment L526 and the first commit's message require "≥2 distinct method-types", and the module docstring says confidence is "NEVER a single method — it is a weighted sum of independent votes"; in the code `swing_pivots` and `multi_tf` are always present, both derived from the swing pivots, so the method-type condition is always met with the committed weights, every group present on all three timeframes is HIGH, and a two-timeframe group with three touches is HIGH, with no volume, prior-day, round-number or recency vote (P01-S076 to P01-S080, P01-S121, P01-S124, P01-S125). - P01-AP02 — Plateaus and outside bars: SR-P1's parenthesis says a "perfectly flat plateau does not mark every bar", and the docstring says a bar yields both a HIGH and a LOW only "in degenerate single-bar windows"; in the code every bar of a plateau with lower bars around it becomes a pivot, and an outside bar yields both, so touches can count one swing several times (P01-S041 to P01-S043, P01-S056, P01-S122). - P01-AP03 — Width of a zone and of a merged group: clustering chains without a width limit (as SR-Z1 states), the merge compares with a drifting group mean, and the merge joins zones of one timeframe too, against SR-C1's "ACROSS timeframes"; the merged band is the union, gaps included (P01-S053, P01-S072, P01-S074, P01-S120).
