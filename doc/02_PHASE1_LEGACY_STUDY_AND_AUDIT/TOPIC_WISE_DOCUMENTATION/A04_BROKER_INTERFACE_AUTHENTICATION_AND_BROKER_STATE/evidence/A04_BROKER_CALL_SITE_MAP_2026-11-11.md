# A04 evidence: broker call-site map

**Status:** DRAFT — evidence for the A04 STUDY, for ChatGPT's review
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Revision history:** First version, 2026-11-11: written by a read-only helper as STUDY part P04, checked statement by statement by a separate helper, and corrected by a third pass.
**Purpose and scope:** The complete map of the non-test callers of the broker interface at the baseline: the public surface of `ZerodhaAdapter` and its result and exception contract as callers receive it; how the single adapter instance and the Kite client are constructed, held and injected; every non-test call site of the adapter's public methods (and of the private members that callers reach), with its owner topic, purpose and handling of the result and of exceptions; every direct use of the Kite client, of Kite HTTP endpoints and of the broker token file outside `broker/zerodha_adapter.py` (including scripts and the dashboard); the callers of the other broker classes; and the duplicated wrappers, client objects, token readers, status mappings, product mappings, rate-limit rules and cost calculations (direction questions 10 and 22). Tests are A19's and are counted only. The owners' business logic is not audited here; the adapter's internals are parts P01 to P03, the rate limiter P05, the order monitor and state machine P06, authentication and the clock-skew probe P07, token tooling P08, products and position helpers P09, costs and slippage P10, and the prior-topic crosswalk P11. Statement IDs `P04-Snnn` are kept for traceability.
**Source:** trading-system-prod-snapshot, branch `main`, commit `970aabf3c78d777d9d34549e021f7c8e756c7bdb`, read from the local read-only clone. Nothing in the clone was written or run. No credential, token or account value is reproduced (D-07).
