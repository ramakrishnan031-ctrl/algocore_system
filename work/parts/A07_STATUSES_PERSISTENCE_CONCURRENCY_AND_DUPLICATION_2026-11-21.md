- P09-S020 FACT (the store's transaction unit, A03's, interface only): every store method that A07 calls opens its own `with self.transaction()` (`update_signal_status` L1422; `insert_screener_result` L1930 to L1961; `insert_gate_state` L2961; `release_gate_state` L2982 to L2998; `clear_all_gate_state` L3000 to L3007; `get_all_gate_state` L3009 to L3014; P03-S078, P06-S038, P06-S031, P06-S062, P06-S072); `transaction()` yields a cursor inside an explicit transaction that "Commits on successful exit, rolls back on any exception" (`core/state_store.py` L506 to L520), opened with `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` (P07-S026, citing L506 to L540); each connection is opened with `isolation_level=None`, `check_same_thread=True` and `timeout=30` (L226 to L240) and carries `PRAGMA busy_timeout = 30000` (P03-S095, citing L112).
