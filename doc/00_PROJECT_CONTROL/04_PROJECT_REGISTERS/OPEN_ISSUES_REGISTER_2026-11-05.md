## How entries are recorded

- **Issue ID:** `<TOPIC_ID>-NN`. A01's numbers follow the order of the A01 AUDIT's section 10(b); A02's follow the order of the A02 AUDIT's section 12(b). Each heading keeps the AUDIT's own issue IDs and the STUDY's R numbers, so that every entry can be traced to its AUDIT record.
- **Three kinds of statement are kept apart** in each entry's Resolution field:
  1. FACT / HISTORICAL: what the legacy repository establishes, taken from the AUDIT and its evidence file, and never changed to agree with an answer;
  2. Rama's statement about the legacy system: how it was operated, or what it was meant to do (an intent source under Document 04, section 5.1, with its date);
  3. Rama's treatment for the new system: input to the Phase 2 proposal (Document 04, section 5.5). It is not an approved V2 design decision, and no legacy behaviour becomes a V2 requirement automatically.
- **Recommendations** by ChatGPT or Claude are labelled as such and stay PROPOSED until Rama decides them (Document 05, section 3).
- **Status** has two parts. History (what the legacy system was meant to do, or how it was run): ANSWERED BY RAMA, with his statement quoted, or OPEN QUESTION (OPEN FOR RAMA); answers from evidence stay in the AUDIT. Treatment (the new system), with the labels of Document 05, section 3: OPEN QUESTION until Rama gives it; UNDER DISCUSSION while his recorded answer awaits his confirmation of the read-back; AGREED once he has confirmed it. A recording-only entry (A01-25), which records an issue whose treatment Rama left OPEN without a question, shows its history as "not asked". A part of a question that Rama leaves open is shown OPEN. No entry is marked CLOSED without Rama's explicit decision of its treatment (Document 05, sections 8 and 11.3).
- **Where Rama's words are recorded:** the discussion itself is not kept (Document 07, section 3, retention rule), so each entry quotes the words that decide it.
- **Sensitive values:** none. Credentials, tokens and account identifiers are named by type only.
