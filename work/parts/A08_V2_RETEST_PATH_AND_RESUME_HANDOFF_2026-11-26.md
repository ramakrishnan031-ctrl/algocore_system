- P04-S025 INTERPRETATION (the price rules in words; from P04-S024): LONG: a break is a one-minute close strictly above `band_high × (1 + m/100)`, with `m` = `breakout_margin_pct` (at the committed 0.0, any close above the band's top); the wrong-way reject is a close strictly below `band_low × (1 − a/100)`, with `a` = `max_away_pct` (at the committed 1.0, a close more than 1 % below the band's bottom). SHORT: a break is a close strictly below `band_low × (1 − m/100)`; the wrong-way reject is a close strictly above `band_high × (1 + a/100)` (at the committed 1.0, more than 1 % above the band's top). Only closes count for the break and the reject: a high or low beyond a level does not.
- P04-S026 FACT (the fold, L111 to L127): `state = WAIT_BREAKOUT` (L111); for each candle `c` in list order (L112): (1) `if failed(c): return RetestResult(REJECT, fail_reason)` (L113 to L114), in every state; (2) `if state == WAIT_BREAKOUT:` and `broke(c)`, `state = WAIT_RETEST` (L116 to L118); (3) `elif state == WAIT_RETEST:` and `c.low <= band_high and c.high >= band_low`, `state = WAIT_CONFIRM` (L119 to L122; comment L120 "retest touch: the candle's range re-enters the band (side-agnostic)."); (4) `elif state == WAIT_CONFIRM:` and `broke(c) and _strong_close(c, params.confirm_strong_close_frac, is_long)`, `return RetestResult(CONFIRMED, confirm_reason)` (L123 to L125). - P04-S027 INTERPRETATION (the transitions; from P04-S026): (a) a candle makes at most one transition (an `if`/`elif` chain), so the breaking candle cannot also be the touch and the touching candle cannot also confirm: a confirmation needs at least three candles (break, touch, confirming break); (b) there is no backward transition: in `WAIT_RETEST` a candle that does not touch leaves the state as it is, and in `WAIT_CONFIRM` a candle that is not a strong break leaves it there, however far the price moves away, until the timeout or a wrong-way reject; (c) in `WAIT_BREAKOUT` a candle inside the band has no effect (the entry was inside the padded zone at the divert, section 4, so the first candles may well be); (d) the touch is the same test for both sides: the candle's range overlaps the unpadded band, both edges inclusive, with no margin or buffer; (e) the wrong-way test runs before the state step on every candle, so a close past the reject level ends the fold with `REJECT` in any state, after a break or a touch too; (f) a terminal result ends the fold and later candles are not examined. LONG and SHORT differ only in the predicates of P04-S024 and the polarity of `_strong_close`.
- P04-S028 INTERPRETATION and UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (candle order and duplicates; A05 CM-05's A08 side, P04 secondary): the fold applies the transitions in list order; nothing in the function sorts, de-duplicates or checks the times of the candles (it reads no `ts`, P04-S022), and the docstring and the parameter comment assume "chronological" order (L13, L84). - P04-S029 FACT (`_strong_close`, L130 to L137): signature `_strong_close(c, frac: float, is_long: bool = True) -> bool`; docstring "Directional strong close: LONG wants the close in the UPPER frac of the 1m range, SHORT in the LOWER frac."; `rng = c.high - c.low` (L133); `if rng <= 0:` (comment "degenerate (flat) bar") `return c.close >= c.high if is_long else c.close <= c.low` (L134 to L135); otherwise `pos = (c.close - c.low) / rng if is_long else (c.high - c.close) / rng` (L136) and `return pos >= frac` (L137).
- P04-S030 INTERPRETATION (what "strong" means; from P04-S029, with the committed `confirm_strong_close_frac: 0.6`): LONG: the close must be at least 60 % of the way from the low to the high, that is, in the top 40 % of the range; SHORT: the close must be at least 60 % of the way down from the high, in the bottom 40 % of the range. The comments' wording ("UPPER frac", "the strong 60%") is compared with this in section 12. Zero range (L134 to L135): a flat bar whose close equals its high and low is strong for both sides, so in `WAIT_CONFIRM` a flat one-minute bar whose close is past the break level confirms whatever the fraction is; a malformed bar with `high < low` takes the same branch. A NaN in the high, low or close gives a NaN range and position, both comparisons false, so not strong; an infinite high or low gives a position of 0 or NaN, not strong unless the fraction is at or below 0 (P04-S006).
- P04-S031 INTERPRETATION (NaN and infinite candle values in the fold; P04-S006): a NaN close neither breaks nor fails (every comparison is false), so it changes nothing; a NaN low or high makes the touch false. A positive infinite close breaks on the long side (and is not a wrong-way close) and is a wrong-way close (`reclaim_up`) on the short side; a negative infinite close is the mirror. `evaluate` validates no candle value, and `Candle.from_kite` passes NaN and infinite prices through (P01-S017).
- P04-S032 INTERPRETATION (bands and parameters outside the committed values; from P04-S024, P04-S019): `evaluate` does not check `band_low <= band_high` or that the parameters are positive. - P04-S033 INTERPRETATION ("pure, idempotent and restart-safe", L12 to L18): `evaluate` is a pure function of its arguments (no clock, no state, P04-S012), so the same arguments give the same result, as the tests state (`tests/unit/test_sr_v2_retest_confirm.py` L100 to L102, L169 to L171). The result therefore depends only on the candle list that the caller supplies at each evaluation and on the elapsed time; "restart-safe" holds as far as the same candles since the divert can be fetched again after a restart (the monitor's window, section 6), and a different list (a fetch failure, a forming candle completed, a broker correction) can give a different result.

The three writes are separate store calls, each its own transaction (A03 P-11's A08 side, section 11).
- P04-S047 FACT (logging by the diverter): it writes a line only for an exception (L377) and for a failed audit write (L433); the divert's INFO line is the processor's (L1052 to L1055) and the parking INFO line the monitor's `register` (L98 to L102). `_safe_log` (L435 to L441) does nothing without a logger and swallows any exception from the logger call.
