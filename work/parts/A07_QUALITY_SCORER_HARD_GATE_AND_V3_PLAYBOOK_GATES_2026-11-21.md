### 3.2 `score()`: the arithmetic

- P05-S018 FACT (per step, L79 to L103): for each `name` in `self._step_names`, `weight: int = getattr(steps_cfg, name)` (L87) and `total_weights_possible += weight` (L88). If `name not in step_results`, one WARNING is logged ("quality_scorer.missing_step: step '%s' missing - excluded from scoring denominator", L92 to L95), the name is appended to `missing_steps` and `step_scores[name] = 0` (L96 to L97, comment "still report 0 in step_scores for transparency"). Otherwise `raw = float(step_results[name])` (L99), `weighted = raw * weight` (L100), `step_scores[name] = int(round(weighted))` (L101), `total_achieved += weighted` (L102) and `total_weights_present += weight` (L103). A key of `step_results` that is not one of the step names is ignored (the loop runs over the names only).
- P05-S019 FACT (the total, L105 to L120): if `total_weights_present > 0`, `final_score = (total_achieved / total_weights_present) * 100.0` (L107) and `total_score = min(100, int(round(final_score)))` (L108), then one INFO line "quality_scorer.proportional_scoring: effective_weights=%d / total_weights=%d (%.1f%%)" whose third value is `(total_weights_present / total_weights_possible) * 100.0` (L109 to L114); otherwise `total_score = 0` and one WARNING "quality_scorer.all_steps_missing: returning score 0 (no valid steps)" (L115 to L120). The total is capped at 100 and has no floor.
- P05-S020 INTERPRETATION and current external context (P05-S006): `round()` without `ndigits` rounds half to even and returns an integer, so at L108 a proportional value of 59.5 gives 60, 60.5 gives 60 and 64.5 gives 64, and at L101 a weighted step value of 2.5 gives 2 and 7.5 gives 8. The total is computed from the unrounded weighted values (L102, L107), so the sum of the rounded `step_scores` need not equal `total_score`. Units: each raw value is a step score (P04 section 7; nominally 0.0 to 1.0, QS3), each weight an integer number of points; the total is a percentage of the weights present.
- P05-S021 FACT (tier and `passed`, L122 to L134): `high_thr = self._weights.high_score_threshold`, `med_thr = self._weights.medium_score_threshold` (L122 to L123); `total_score >= high_thr` gives `"HIGH"` and increments `_fx_tier_high` (L124 to L128), else `total_score >= med_thr` gives `"MEDIUM"` (L129 to L130), else `"LOW"` (L131 to L132); `passed = total_score >= self._weights.min_pass_score` (L134). All three comparisons are inclusive. The result carries `min_pass_score=self._weights.min_pass_score` and `tier_thresholds={"high": high_thr, "medium": med_thr}` (L142 to L150).
- P05-S022 FACT (telemetry, L136 to L140): `_fx_score.inc()` on every call that reaches L138, and `_fx_ceiling.inc()` when `total_score > _G2_CEILING_TRIPWIRE`, a module constant 65 (L19) whose comment reads "the G2 achievable ceiling — 25/100 pts dead-at-0 + 20 pinned-at-half make >65 algebraically impossible today. A score above it means G2's dead inputs came alive" (L16 to L18); the HIGH-tier comment reads "unreachable today (IA-P2-03: threshold 80 > ceiling 65)" (L126 to L127). The counters change no result.

### 3.3 Failure behaviour and side effects

- P05-S023 FACT: the scorer has no `try`, no `except` and no `raise` statement (reading L1 to L150). Every attribute read of the configuration, every `float()` conversion, every `round()` and both divisions (L107, L113) are unguarded; an exception raised there leaves `score()`.
- P05-S024 INTERPRETATION (what can raise on the screener's route, and what follows): on the OFF and shadow paths `step_results` is the executor's dictionary, in which every value is a float that the executor stores (a step's return value, 0.0 for an ERROR step, 0.5 for a TIMEOUT step; P04-S034 to P04-S036), so `float()` at L99 does not raise; a NaN or infinite value would make `int(round(...))` raise at L101 or L108 (CPython's `PyLong_FromDouble`, P05-S006), and none of the step routes recorded by P04 (section 7) returns such a value from finite quote fields. The division in the INFO line (L113) is by zero only if the configured weights sum to 0 while a present weight is positive, which requires a negative weight; the schema has no sign check on the weights (P05-S032) and every committed weight is positive (P05-S031). Any exception that leaves `score()` is caught by the screener and becomes `SKIPPED_SCORER_ERROR` with an ERROR line and traceback (`screening/secondary_screener.py` L289 to L300; P03-S062).
- P05-S025 FACT and INTERPRETATION (side effects): the scorer reads no store, broker, file or clock and writes none; its effects are the log lines of P05-S018 and P05-S019 (one INFO line on every call that has any step present, so one per screening on the OFF and shadow paths, and one WARNING per missing step) and the counter increments of P05-S021 and P05-S022 on process-wide objects (`EffectCounter.inc` is `self.n += 1`, without a lock, `core/effect_telemetry.py` L84 to L87, comment "Cannot raise"). INTERPRETATION: QS9's "Pure function ... Thread-safe" (P05-S009) holds for the returned value (the same `step_results` and configuration give the same `ScoreResult`); the log lines and counter increments are side effects; whether concurrent increments from several processor workers can lose counts is A15's matter and part P09's (Q23).

### 7.3 What the V3 total can reach on the live route

### 8.2 `gate_rr` (L240 to L294): the stop, target and R:R arithmetic, recorded as written

- P05-S072 FACT (signature): `gate_rr(side: str, entry: float, *, sl_zone_edge: Optional[float], tgt_zone_edge: Optional[float], atr30: Optional[float], rr_floor: float, sl_buffer_atr_mult: float) -> GateVerdict` (L240 to L249); no parameter has a default.

- P05-S074 FACT (buffer, stop, target, risk and reward, L271 to L281): `buffer = (float(sl_buffer_atr_mult) * float(atr30)) if atr30 else 0.0` (L271). If `_is_long(side)`: `sl = float(sl_zone_edge) - buffer`, `tgt = float(tgt_zone_edge)`, `risk = entry - sl`, `reward = tgt - entry` (L272 to L276). Otherwise: `sl = float(sl_zone_edge) + buffer`, `tgt = float(tgt_zone_edge)`, `risk = sl - entry`, `reward = entry - tgt` (L277 to L281). `entry` is used as given (it is not passed through `float()`).
- P05-S075 FACT (evidence and geometry, L282 to L286): `ev = {"v3_sl": round(sl, 4), "v3_tgt": round(tgt, 4)}` (L282); `if risk <= 0 or reward <= 0` the detail "invalid geometry (risk=..., reward=...)" (four decimals) and `ev["v3_rr"] = None` are added and the verdict is `GateVerdict(False, "RR", ev)` (L283 to L286).
- P05-S076 FACT (the ratio and the floor, L287 to L294): `rr = reward / risk` (L287); `ev["v3_rr"] = round(rr, 4)` (L288); `if rr >= float(rr_floor)` the verdict is `GateVerdict(True, None, ev)` (L289 to L290); otherwise the detail "R:R {rr:.2f} < floor {rr_floor}" is added and the verdict is `GateVerdict(False, "RR", ev)` (L291 to L292). Any exception gives `GateVerdict(False, "RR", {"detail": f"error: {exc}"})` (L293 to L294; comment "a gate must never raise into the shadow worker").
- P05-S077 INTERPRETATION (meaning and units, from P05-S073 to P05-S076): for a long side the stop is the support-side edge supplied as `sl_zone_edge` lowered by the buffer and the target is the resistance-side edge supplied as `tgt_zone_edge`, with no buffer; for any other side value (`"SELL"`, `"SHORT"`, `None`, an empty or unknown text) the mirror arithmetic applies: the stop is `sl_zone_edge` raised by the buffer and the target is `tgt_zone_edge`. The buffer is `sl_buffer_atr_mult` times `atr30`, so `entry`, the two edges, `atr30`, the buffer, the stop and the target are all in the price unit of the caller's inputs, and `rr_floor` and `sl_buffer_atr_mult` are dimensionless. The gate selects no zone and checks only that risk and reward are both positive: the caller decides which band edges are passed (section 9; A18), A08 produces the zones, and the entry, stop, target, risk and R:R decisions for live trades stay A09's (D-39, P1). The gate does not round to a tick, does not test the circuit band and does not compare with the live stop or target.
- P05-S078 INTERPRETATION (worked figures, the arithmetic of L271 to L289 at the inputs of A19's tests `tests/unit/test_v3_chain.py` L116 to L138; the tests assert only pass or fail and `v3_rr > 2.0`): `gate_rr("BUY", 100.0, sl_zone_edge=95.0, tgt_zone_edge=115.0, atr30=2.0, rr_floor=2.0, sl_buffer_atr_mult=0.20)` gives buffer 0.4, stop 94.6, risk 5.4, reward 15.0, R:R 2.7778 (rounded to four places): PASS; with `tgt_zone_edge=104.0`, reward 4.0 and R:R 0.7407: FAIL "RR"; `gate_rr("SELL", 100.0, sl_zone_edge=105.0, tgt_zone_edge=85.0, ...)` gives stop 105.4, risk 5.4, reward 15.0, R:R 2.7778: PASS; `gate_rr("BUY", 100.0, sl_zone_edge=101.0, tgt_zone_edge=115.0, atr30=0.0, ...)` gives buffer 0.0, stop 101.0, risk −1.0: FAIL "invalid geometry".
- P05-S079 INTERPRETATION (boundaries and display): an R:R equal to the floor passes (`>=`, L289); the comparison uses the unrounded ratio, the evidence carries it rounded to four places and the detail text to two places, so a failing ratio of 1.996 against a floor of 2.0 is described as "R:R 2.00 < floor 2.0" (Python formatting, P05-S006 local check). A risk or reward of exactly 0 is an invalid geometry, not a ratio.
- P05-S080 INTERPRETATION (missing and malformed inputs; NaN behaviour from P05-S006): a missing (`None`) or zero `atr30` gives a buffer of 0 and is not a failure (the docstring says "0 when ATR30 is unavailable", L256); a negative `atr30` or `sl_buffer_atr_mult` gives a negative buffer, which moves the stop toward or past the zone edge, with no sign test; a NaN `atr30` is truthy and gives a NaN buffer, stop, risk and ratio; a NaN `entry` passes `entry <= 0` (false) and also gives NaN risk, reward and ratio; in each NaN case the geometry test (`<= 0`) is false and `rr >= rr_floor` is false, so the verdict is a FAIL "RR" with the floor text (the local interpreter returns `nan` for `round(nan, 4)`, P05-S006); an infinite `entry` makes the reward (long) or the risk (short) negative infinity, an invalid geometry FAIL; an `entry` that cannot be compared with 0 (for example a text) raises at L264 and takes the error route, FAIL "error: ...". A `side` of `"SELL"` or any text other than `BUY` or `LONG` (after stripping and upper-casing) takes the short arithmetic.
- P05-S081 HISTORICAL and FACT (the docstring, L250 to L262, against the code): the docstring's formulas ("LONG : SL = support_edge − buffer (below support); TGT = resistance_edge above. SHORT : SL = resistance_edge + buffer (above resistance); TGT = support_edge below. buffer = sl_buffer_atr_mult × ATR30 (0 when ATR30 is unavailable). Units: prices for the level, the lows and the ATR; `proximity_pct` multiplies the level directly (a fraction: 0.005 is 0.5 % of the level), and the two ATR multipliers are dimensionless. An `atr30` of 0 is not "missing": it gives `proximity = proximity_pct × level` and a hold floor equal to the level; a negative `atr30` puts the hold floor above the level. Unlike `gate_confirm`, NaN inputs fail here: a NaN level or session low makes `touched` false, and a NaN lowest close or ATR makes `held` false (the hold floor is NaN), because a pass needs both comparisons true (P05-S006). Like `gate_confirm` the gate is long-only by construction (support below, close above the floor).
