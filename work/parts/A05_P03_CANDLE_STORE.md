## 2. The candle record (`CandleData`) and the accumulator (`_Accumulator`)

P03-S009. HISTORICAL (`data/candle_store.py` L1 to L10): the module docstring calls the file a "Minute OHLC candle builder (LF9-LF18)" that "Builds minute candles from LTP ticks" and is the "Single source of truth for intraday candle data"; its "Audit 3.4 fixes" are "Candle OHLC built from LTP only (never from tick["ohlc"])" and "Candle close triggered by clock thread, not by tick arrival".

P03-S010. FACT (L12 to L21): the imports are `from __future__ import annotations`, the standard modules `threading`, `time`, `collections.deque`, `dataclasses.dataclass`, `datetime.datetime` and `timedelta`, `typing`, and `now_ist` from `core.time_authority`. No broker library, database, configuration, event-bus or rounding module is imported.

P03-S011. FACT (L24 to L36): `CandleData` is a `@dataclass(frozen=True)` with the fields `instrument_token: int`, `symbol: str`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close` (each `float`), `volume: int`, `ts: datetime`, `interval_sec: int` and `is_synthetic: bool = False`. HISTORICAL (field comments): `volume` "FIX-135 Item 46: accumulated from ticks (was always 0)" (L33); `ts` "IST candle close time (from clock thread)" (L34); `is_synthetic` "FIX-020: True for flat carry-forward candles" (L36); class docstring "LF13: Immutable candle record emitted on candle close" (L26).

P03-S012. INTERPRETATION (Python dataclass semantics): `frozen=True` makes assignment to a field of an emitted candle raise `FrozenInstanceError`; the dataclass checks no types and no values at construction, so the annotations are not enforced.

P03-S013. FACT (L39 to L48): `_Accumulator` is a mutable `@dataclass` with `open`, `high`, `low`, `close` (floats), `tick_count: int`, `window_start: datetime` (comment "FIX-049: minute boundary for this accumulator", L47) and `volume: int = 0` (comment "FIX-135 Item 46: accumulated volume from ticks", L48).

P03-S014. FACT (L50 to L63): `_Accumulator.update(ltp, volume=0)` sets `high = ltp` when `ltp > high` (L57 to L58), `low = ltp` when `ltp < low` (L59 to L60), `close = ltp` (L61), adds 1 to `tick_count` (L62) and adds the `volume` argument to `volume` (L63). `open` is never changed after the accumulator is created. HISTORICAL (docstring L51 to L56): the FIX-049 `exchange_timestamp` late-tick discard "was removed (it was dead — never wired — and a landmine ...). Every tick routed here is accepted; window assignment is clock-driven upstream."

P03-S015. INTERPRETATION (code reading of L50 to L63 and L187 to L195) (B3): for one token and one interval, open is the first LTP routed to the store after the previous close (or after the last `mark_reconnect`), high and low are the maximum and minimum of the routed LTPs, close is the last routed LTP, and volume is the sum of the `volume` arguments. "First" and "last" are in the order of the `on_tick` calls, not in exchange-time order. The store treats the `volume` argument as a per-tick increment: it adds whatever value it is given and computes no difference between successive values.

P03-S016. FACT (grep of `window_start` and `\btick_count\b` in non-test Python: only `data/candle_store.py` L46, L47, L62, L188, L193, L194): `window_start` and `tick_count` are written but never read; neither is copied into `CandleData` (L316 to L328).

P03-S017. FACT (grep of `round\(|Decimal|tick_size|math\.|isnan|isfinite` in `data/candle_store.py`: no match; L50 to L63, L314 to L347) (B7, I5): the store does no rounding, no tick-size snapping and no precision handling, and checks no price for sign, zero or finiteness. Prices are compared and copied as the floats it is given; a synthetic candle copies the previous candle's close unchanged (L287, L338 to L341). Tick-size inputs are not used by the store. Upstream, the published library divides the packet integer by the segment divisor (part P02, P02-S048) and the feed applies `float()` (part P01, P01-S041).

## 4. Tick intake: `on_tick`

P03-S029. FACT (L170 to L195): `on_tick(instrument_token, ltp, ts, volume=0)` takes the lock, computes `window_start = now_ist().replace(second=0, microsecond=0)` (L188), and either calls `update(ltp, volume)` on the token's existing accumulator (L189 to L190) or creates one with `open = high = low = close = ltp`, `tick_count = 1`, that `window_start` and the given `volume` (L191 to L195). The `ts` parameter is not used anywhere in the body (L187 to L195).

P03-S030. HISTORICAL (docstring L171 to L186): "LF11: Accumulate LTP + volume into the current (clock-driven) candle window. Audit 3.4: exchange OHLC from the tick is NEVER used here. FIX-135 Item 46: volume accumulated from ticks." It records that the FIX-049 `exchange_timestamp` late-tick discard was removed by H-9 (Wave-3) because "the live tick dispatcher never passed exchange_timestamp, so it never ran" and, with the `_last_closed_window` off-by-one, it was a "LANDMINE"; and that `ts` "(the received/exchange tick time) is retained for the caller interface but is INFORMATIONAL ONLY — it neither gates nor windows anything."

P03-S031. INTERPRETATION (code reading of L187 to L195, L269 to L271) (B2, B4, A4): which candle a tick belongs to is decided only by the order in which the dispatcher's `on_tick` and the timer's snapshot (L269 to L271) take the lock: a tick counts in the candle of the first close that takes its snapshot after the tick's `on_tick` call. Neither the exchange time, nor the feed's receipt time, nor the `ts` argument affects it. A tick received before a boundary but dispatched after the timer's snapshot, for example one still waiting in the feed's queue (part P01, P01-S047), is counted in the next candle.

P03-S032. INTERPRETATION (code reading of L50 to L63, L187 to L195) (B4, A4): the store has no handling of duplicate, late or out-of-order ticks: a repeated tick is applied again (and its `volume` argument added again); an out-of-order tick becomes the close if it is the last one routed in the interval; no tick is discarded. FACT (grep of `window_start`, P03-S016): the `window_start` value computed at L188 is stored only in a new accumulator and never compared with anything.

P03-S033. INTERPRETATION (code reading of L187 to L195): `on_tick` validates nothing: it does not check that the token is in the token map, that `ltp` is positive or finite, or that `volume` is non-negative. A token missing from the map still gets an accumulator and, at the close, a real candle whose `symbol` is `str(token)` (L315). In the wired path the feed's consumer drops ticks whose token is None or whose price is None or 0.0 (part P01, P01-S048), and the feed converts the price with `float()` (part P01, P01-S041).

P03-S034. INTERPRETATION (Python semantics): a non-comparable `ltp` (for example None) would raise `TypeError` at L57 when a second tick reaches an existing accumulator; the `with` block releases the lock and the exception propagates to the caller (in the wired system, the tick dispatcher; part P01, P01-S056). The feed's filtering (P03-S033) keeps such values away from the dispatcher.

## 5. The clock and the close: `_timer_loop` and `_close_candles`

P03-S035. FACT (L233 to L251): `_timer_loop` repeats while `_stop_event` is clear: it reads `now_ts = time.time()` (L242), computes `next_fire = ((now_ts // interval) + 1) * interval` (L243 to L245), sets `sleep_sec = max(0.0, next_fire - now_ts)` (L246 to L248), waits with `_stop_event.wait(sleep_sec)` and breaks if the event was set (L249 to L250), and otherwise calls `_close_candles()` (L251). There is no try in the loop. HISTORICAL (docstring L234 to L240): "Clock-aligned candle close ... FIX-080: Uses time.time() for wall-clock boundary alignment (candle close timing must match market time), but guards sleep_sec with max(0, x) to prevent ValueError on negative sleep from NTP step or leap second."

P03-S036. INTERPRETATION (arithmetic; `core/time_authority.py` L58; `main.py` L2942): the boundaries are whole multiples of the interval in epoch seconds. With the interval of 60 s that `main.py` passes, they fall on IST minute boundaries, because the IST offset (5 h 30 min = 19,800 s) is a whole number of minutes. An interval that does not divide 19,800 s (for example 3,600 s) would give boundaries that are not IST clock multiples; no non-test code passes another interval.

P03-S037. INTERPRETATION (code reading of L241 to L251, L262) (B2): the next boundary is recomputed from the clock after every close, so drift does not accumulate; but the loop neither checks after waking that the boundary has been reached nor records which boundary it last closed. Consequences: a late wake stamps the candle late (P03-S045); if a close and its callbacks take longer than the interval, the next boundary is skipped and the following candle covers more than one interval under one time stamp; a wake before the boundary (for example after a backward wall-clock step during the wait) runs one close early and a second close at the boundary, so the same minute is split into two candles. The clock that `Event.wait` uses for its timeout depends on the Python version, which is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A01 AUDIT, B-41). Whether any of these cases occurred is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED.

P03-S038. FACT (L262 to L266, L272 to L278): `_close_candles()` first reads `close_ts = now_ist()` once, outside the lock (L262), and sets `window_being_closed = close_ts.replace(second=0, microsecond=0)` (L266). Under the lock it sets `_last_closed_window = window_being_closed - timedelta(seconds=interval)` (L276 to L278). HISTORICAL (comments L263 to L265, L272 to L275, H-9): the timer "fires AT the boundary (e.g. 10:01:00) to close the window that just ENDED (10:00)"; the off-by-one fix is "Harmless today (no live reader) but correct". FACT (grep of `_last_closed_window`: L97, L276, L305 to L306 only): the value is read only in the warning text at L305 to L306.

P03-S039. FACT (`core/time_authority.py` L13, L58 to L59, L94 to L99): `now_ist()` returns `datetime.now(tz=_IST)`, where `_IST = timezone(timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30), name="IST")`: an aware datetime with a fixed +05:30 offset, read from the host clock. HISTORICAL (L13): "now_ist() returns the VM's current time in IST — the runtime clock." The store therefore uses two readings of the same host clock: `time.time()` to schedule the close and `now_ist()` to stamp it and to compute `window_start`.

P03-S040. FACT (L268 to L287) (section 1 of the close, under the lock): the store copies `_accum` into `to_close` and clears `_accum` (L270 to L271), copies the token map (L279), forms `tokens_with_no_ticks` as the mapped tokens without an accumulator in this interval (L281), and for each of them with a non-empty history records the close of the last history candle as a synthetic candidate (L282 to L287). HISTORICAL (docstring L256 to L257): "FIX-020: If a token had no ticks this minute BUT has previous history, emit a synthetic flat candle: O=H=L=C=prev_close, V=0, is_synthetic=True."

P03-S041. INTERPRETATION (code reading of L281 to L287, L331 to L347) (B3): for an interval with no tick, a mapped token with history gets one synthetic flat candle (open, high, low and close equal to the previous close, volume 0, `is_synthetic` True); a token that has never had a real candle in this process gets nothing; a token that has history but is not in the token map gets nothing (the candidate set comes from the map). A token's first candle in a process is therefore always a real one. Synthetic candles chain: each is appended to the history, so the next interval's synthetic candle repeats the same price, and so on without limit while the process runs.

P03-S042. FACT (L289 to L308): the synthetic-only guard, under the same lock: when any real accumulator closed, `_consecutive_synthetic_only` is reset to 0 (L294 to L295); otherwise, when synthetic candidates exist, it is increased by 1 and a WARNING is logged when it equals 3 or is a multiple of 30, naming the count, `_last_closed_window` and the number of carry-forward tokens, with the text "tick feed may be starved/misconfigured; candles are synthetic" (L296 to L308); when there are neither real nor synthetic candles the counter is unchanged and nothing is logged. P03-S046. FACT (L349 to L351): when there is no real and no synthetic candle, the close returns at once: no history change and no callback.

P03-S048. FACT (L362 to L368): outside the lock, for each candle in the order of P03-S044, each callback of the copied list is called in registration order, each call in its own `try/except Exception` that logs ERROR "CandleStore: callback raised: %s" with the exception's text (no traceback). P03-S049. P03-S050. INTERPRETATION (Python semantics; L233 to L251, L253 to L360): `_timer_loop` has no try, so an exception raised inside `_close_candles` outside the per-callback try ends the timer thread, and nothing restarts it (P03-S023). ## 12. Candle flows, finality and the live side of live-against-historical consistency

P03-S101. FACT and UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (B1; sections 4, 5 and 8; part P01, P01-S038): the component that turns ticks into candles is `data/candle_store.py` `CandleStore`, with one interval, 60 s, in the wired system. It builds candles only from ticks that arrive through the dispatcher; whether ticks arrived depends on subscriptions. No token is subscribed at boot. A later subscription depends on the exit-retry path. Whether that path ever ran in production is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED.

P03-S102. INTERPRETATION (G3, normal flow; P03-S029, P03-S035 to P03-S048): once ticks for a token arrive, each 60-second close emits one real candle for every token that had a tick in the interval and one synthetic candle for every mapped token with history and no tick; each candle goes into memory (last 500 per token) and to `smart_tgt._on_candle_close`, then (when on) the structure-exit callback, then `_persist_candle`, in that order, on the timer thread.

P03-S103. INTERPRETATION (G3, degraded flows; P03-S041 to P03-S043, P03-S060): when no tick has ever arrived in a process, the timer still fires every minute but emits nothing, and no warning is logged; when ticks stop after some real candles, every mapped token with history gets a synthetic candle each minute, and the guard warns after 3 such closes and every 30 after that. The store has no market-hours or session check (FACT: grep of `market|session|hours|09:15|15:30|holiday|weekday` in the file finds only the words "market time" in the docstring at L238): if the process runs after the close of trading, synthetic candles continue every minute for every token with history until `stop()`, and after 500 of them a token's real candles have all been pushed out of memory (P03-S047). How long the process ran each day is A01's subject and, at runtime, UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED.

P03-S104. INTERPRETATION (G3, error flows; P03-S049, P03-S050, P03-S069): a failing close callback is logged and skipped; a failing insert is logged at DEBUG and the close continues; an exception outside the callback try ends the timer thread without restart, after which no candle is emitted and the accumulators grow without being closed.

P03-S105. INTERPRETATION (G3, disconnect and reconnect flow; P03-S056, P03-S057, P03-S089; part P01, P01-S136): the timer keeps firing while the feed is disconnected, so every minute of a gap gives each mapped token with history a synthetic candle; at the first reconnect attempt the open accumulators are discarded and `smart_tgt` recomputes from history (synthetic candles included); after reconnection real candles resume, the first one possibly partial. Missed minutes are not refilled from any other source, and nothing in the candles marks the gap other than `is_synthetic`.

P03-S106. INTERPRETATION (G3, restart flow; P03-S010, P03-S075): history lives only in memory, so a new process starts with no candles; it emits nothing until the first real tick, and synthetic candles only after a token's first real candle. The store never reads back rows that `_persist_candle` or any other writer stored.

P03-S107. INTERPRETATION (G3, stale data; P03-S041, P03-S053): the store has no per-token staleness check. A synthetic candle repeats the last real close indefinitely; a consumer can tell it only by `is_synthetic` (which no consumer reads, P03-S094) or by comparing `ts` with earlier candles. The feed's whole-connection tick-age watchdog is part P01's subject.

P03-S108. FACT and INTERPRETATION (B5; L24 to L36, L353 to L368): a candle is final when its close emits it: the `CandleData` is frozen, appended once to the history and passed once to each callback; the store has no path to revise, correct or re-emit a candle, and a late tick goes into the next candle (P03-S031). The candle covering the interval in which `stop()` or `mark_reconnect` is called is never emitted (P03-S022, P03-S055).

P03-S109. INTERPRETATION (B6, live side only; the historical side is part P06's): the live candle's conventions are: one minute interval aligned to IST minute boundaries by the host clock; membership of a tick by dispatch order, not by exchange time (P03-S031); `ts` the end-of-interval host time, with microseconds (P03-S045); OHLC from LTP only; volume 0 for LTP-mode ticks (P03-S066); synthetic flat candles for silent minutes (P03-S041); no session filter (P03-S103). Whether these match the historical candles (time-stamp convention, volume meaning, gaps, flat candles) is for part P06 and the AUDIT; this part records only the live side.

## 14. Points the AUDIT may examine (neutral)

1. Volume: the store adds the per-tick `volume` argument (P03-S015); the dispatcher passes the feed's `volume_traded` value when present and 0 otherwise (P03-S064 to P03-S066); the current vendor page calls that field "Volume traded for the day" (P03-S072). The sources disagree on intent: LF11 "volume = 0 ... not tracked" (P03-S114), the FIX-135 comments "accumulated from ticks" (P03-S011), the tests' per-tick increments (P03-S112) and the M-D1 record "HELD" (P03-S116).
2. The meaning and form of the live `ts`: an end-of-interval host time with microseconds, the same for all candles of a close (P03-S045, P03-S079), against the schema comment "ISO-8601 IST candle close" and the other writers' forms (A03 Y-06; P03-S082).
3. Window membership by dispatch order rather than exchange or receipt time; the unused `ts` argument and `window_start` field (P03-S016, P03-S029 to P03-S032).
4. Close timing: no check that the boundary was reached and no record of the last boundary closed, so a long close can merge intervals and an early wake can split one (P03-S037).
5. Synthetic candles: carried forward without limit and without a session check, stored in history (where they can push out real candles), passed to every consumer, none of which reads `is_synthetic`, and persisted by the live writer (P03-S041, P03-S047, P03-S094, P03-S103).
6. The synthetic-only guard does not react to a feed that never delivers a tick (P03-S043).
7. The timer thread has no exception guard, no health check and no restart (P03-S023, P03-S050).
8. Close callbacks run synchronously on the timer thread, including a database insert per candle; callback errors are logged without a traceback and insert errors at DEBUG only (P03-S048, P03-S069, P03-S078).
9. `stop()` neither joins nor emits the open candles, and the shutdown closes the store after the candle store is only signalled to stop (P03-S022, P03-S073, P03-S074).
10. The token map is a boot-time copy of every cached instrument, never refreshed; the FIX-092 clean-up chain is unwired (P03-S025, P03-S027, P03-S095).
11. Reconnect: the open candles are discarded at the first reconnect attempt, the next candle may be partial and is not marked, gap minutes become synthetic candles, and nothing is refilled (P03-S056, P03-S057, P03-S105).
12. `start()` has no guard against a second call (P03-S021); `get_candles` returns the whole history for `n=0` (P03-S054; no caller does this).
13. The lock is held while every mapped token is scanned once a minute (P03-S060).
14. Documents against code: LF11 (volume), LF12 (no synthetic candles), LF13 (nine fields) and LF14 (390) against the code (P03-S114); the manager registry's line references (P03-S084); the breach monitor's docstring claiming dispatcher wiring (P03-S092).
15. The candle interval is a literal in `main.py`, not a setting (P03-S062, P03-S063).

## 16. Draft questions about intent for Rama

1. Was a live candle's `volume` meant to be the quantity traded within that minute, a running total for the day, or not tracked at all (LF11 says "not tracked"; the FIX-135 comments say "accumulated from ticks")?
2. Was a live candle's time stamp meant to mark the start of its minute or its end, and was it meant to be the exact minute boundary or the moment the close ran?
3. Was a tick meant to be placed in a minute by the exchange's time, by the time the system received it, or by the time it was processed?
4. Were synthetic flat candles meant to continue without limit for a token that has gone silent, including outside market hours, or only within the session or for a limited time?
5. Were the candle consumers (the SL trail and the structure exit) meant to treat synthetic candles the same as real ones?
6. After a feed reconnect, was the minute in progress meant to be discarded, and were the minutes of the gap meant to be filled with synthetic candles?
7. Was the live candle writer meant to stay dormant while the candle store runs (the "X8: this writer must stay dark" comment), and, if the tick feed were ever wired, which writer was meant to own the `candles` table?
8. Was the in-memory history meant to hold 390 candles (LF14) or 500?
9. Was an instrument refresh during a running process meant to clean up the candle store's state (FIX-092, `gc_sweep`) and to refresh its token-to-symbol map?
