## 8. `shutdown` (A01 C20; A06 PQ-02)

- P03-S090 FACT: `shutdown(self)` (L697 to L705) does one thing: `if hasattr(self._executor, 'shutdown'): self._executor.shutdown()` (L704 to L705). It sets no flag of its own, so the screener does not refuse a later `screen()` call; it does not touch the store, the quote function, the hard gate or the evidence recorder. Docstring L699 to L702: "FIX-100: Shutdown internal step_executor cleanly. Called by signal_processor.stop() during system shutdown."
- P03-S091 FACT (call level; part P04's internals): `StepExecutor.shutdown` (`screening/step_executor.py` L221 to L241) returns at once if already shut down (idempotent), otherwise sets `_executor_shutdown` under `_executor_lock` and calls `shutdown(wait=True, cancel_futures=False)` on the current pool inside a `try` whose `except Exception: pass` swallows any error (L233 to L241). Pools retired by a timeout rotation were shut down with `wait=False` at the rotation (L97 to L112) and are not waited for here.
- P03-S092 FACT (interface, A06's `stop()`): the sole caller is `signals/signal_processor.py` L342 to L344 (`if self._screener is not None and hasattr(self._screener, 'shutdown')`), reached after the dispatcher join with `timeout=10.0` (L335 to L336) and after `self._executor.shutdown(wait=True, cancel_futures=False)` and `self._executor = None` (L338 to L340); `_running = False` follows (L346). `main.py` makes no direct call to the screener's or the step executor's `shutdown` (search of `main.py` for `screener.` and `step_executor.`: no call), although the executor's docstring names "signal_processor.stop() or main.py shutdown sequence" (`screening/step_executor.py` L225).
- P03-S093 INTERPRETATION (with the current external context of P03-S007): `shutdown(wait=True)` on the processor's pool returns only after every submitted run has finished, and `_process_one`, the only caller of `screen()`, is called only from the worker wrapper (`signals/signal_processor.py` L466); so, in the code's order, no screening call from the processor starts after the screener's `shutdown()`.
