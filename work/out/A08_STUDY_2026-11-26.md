## 3. The calculation core: data model, pivots, zones and confluence scoring

**`Candle` and `Candle.from_kite` (A05 CM-10, HA-08, HA-15; section 13).**

- FACT: `Candle` is a frozen dataclass of six fields (`ts`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`) with no validation and no field for the instrument, the interval, the source or whether the bar is complete; its docstring says `ts` is "timezone-aware (or naive IST)" (P01-S014). UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED: what the broker's historical `volume` meant in the legacy period; the vendor page does not define it and A05 keeps it UNKNOWN; a legacy document describes per-interval volume (HISTORICAL) (P01-S014, P01-S138; P01-U10).
- FACT: `from_kite` (L33 to L43) reads exactly `date`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close` by subscription and `volume` by `.get`, converting the prices with `float()` and the volume with `int(row.get("volume") or 0)`; it has no `try`, logs nothing, rounds nothing and reads no tick size; `row["date"]` is stored unchanged, with no parsing or time-zone handling (P01-S015, P01-S016). INTERPRETATION (A05 STUDY section 4, the published `kiteconnect` source): with that library the value is the `datetime` the library parsed, aware when the server's string carried an offset and naive otherwise; whether the legacy server sent aware times throughout is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (P01-S016; P01-U04).
- INTERPRETATION (Python semantics, P01-S004, P01-S005): a missing price key raises `KeyError`, a `None` price `TypeError`, a non-numeric string `ValueError`; NaN and infinity are accepted and stored; no relation between the values is checked (`high < low`, a close outside the range, zero or negative prices all pass) (P01-S017). A missing, `None`, zero or empty volume becomes 0, a fractional one is truncated, `"12.5"` or a NaN volume raises `ValueError`, an infinite one `OverflowError`, and a negative one passes; a consumer cannot tell a missing volume from a zero one (P01-S018).
- FACT: `from_kite` raises to its caller; in `sr_detector/fetch.py` L126 the conversion is inside the `try` of `_fetch_one`, so an exception from any row makes the whole interval's result `None` with one WARNING (section 4); A18's `scripts/forward_shadow_record.py` L212 swallows it (P01-S019). INTERPRETATION: one malformed row discards the whole list for that symbol and interval (P01-S019). FACT: A05's `resample` builds `Candle` objects directly at `core/candle_math.py` L427, so the conversions apply only to candles made by `from_kite` (P01-S020).
- FACT: `from_kite` converts one row and neither sorts nor de-duplicates; `fetch.py` converts the rows in the order the closure returned them (P01-S021). Whether the legacy server's rows were always ascending and unique is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A05 CM-05; P01-S021).

**Swing pivots (`find_swing_pivots`).**

- FACT: the function returns no pivots when `left < 1`, `right < 1` or there are fewer than `left + right + 1` candles, and checks nothing else (P01-S038, P01-S039). For each index from `left` to `n - right - 1` it takes the window of `left + right + 1` bars; a HIGH pivot needs the bar's high to be at least every high in the window and strictly above at least one other bar's; LOW is the mirror; only `high` and `low` are read; the first `left` and last `right` bars are never examined (SR-P1, SR-P2) (P01-S040, P01-S041). Pivots are appended in increasing position, HIGH before LOW at one bar; `bar_index` is the list position, not a time (P01-S044). The function catches nothing and logs nothing (P01-S049).
- INTERPRETATION: every bar of a plateau with lower bars around it becomes a pivot at the same price (highs `[1, 2, 5, 5, 2, 1]` with `n = 2` give two HIGH pivots), and an outside bar yields both a HIGH and a LOW pivot, which the docstring's "only in degenerate single-bar windows" does not describe (P01-S042, P01-S043).
- FACT and INTERPRETATION (input order): the function does not sort and treats list position as time; with the equal `left` and `right` that every non-test caller passes, a reversed series gives the same pivot bars in reversed output order, and any other order gives pivots from positional neighbours that are not time neighbours, with no error; a consumer that reads the output order as time is affected even by a reversed series (P01-S045, P01-S119). INTERPRETATION: a repeated bar can create two pivots and two touches; a missing bar is not detected (P01-S046). A NaN high suppresses every HIGH pivot in a window that contains it, an infinite high can become a pivot, and nothing raises; a non-integer `n` would raise `TypeError`, and every non-test caller passes an integer (P01-S047).
- INTERPRETATION: with the committed `default_pivot_n: 5` (`config/system_config.yaml` L513; committed value only) the newest possible pivot is five bars before the last bar given, on the daily series and on the 60-minute and 30-minute series alike; a still-forming last bar can be a neighbour but not a pivot (P01-S048; P01-S096).

**Zone clustering and the volume profile (`zones.py`).**

- FACT: `cluster_zones` takes the pivots of one timeframe, handles HIGH (to RESISTANCE) then LOW (to SUPPORT), sorts each kind by price and joins a pivot to the running cluster when its price is at most `cluster_pct` per cent above the cluster's highest price; otherwise it closes the cluster; a pivot of another kind is ignored without a log (P01-S050 to P01-S052). `_zone_from_cluster` makes the band `[min, max]` of the cluster's prices, `touches` the number of pivots and `last_touch_ts` the latest pivot `ts` (SR-Z2) (P01-S057).
- INTERPRETATION: because the pivots are sorted, the join test is against the previous pivot, so membership chains without a width limit (pivots 0.4 apart at 100.0 to 102.0 form one zone 2 per cent wide with `cluster_pct` 0.5), as SR-Z1 states (P01-S053). There is no minimum number of touches: a lone pivot is a zone of zero width, against the `Zone` docstring "A price BAND (never a single line)"; only the two buffered membership checks widen a band, for that check only (P01-S054). `touches` counts pivots, not distinct swings, so a plateau, a duplicated bar or an outside bar adds touches (P01-S056). `cluster_pct` of zero or below is not rejected; committed `cluster_pct: 0.5` equals the loader and builder fallbacks (committed value only) (P01-S055). A cluster mixing naive and aware `ts` values would raise `TypeError` in `max`; within one fetched series a mix is not expected (P01-S058).
- FACT: `volume_profile_nodes` buckets each bar's whole volume at its mid-range price into `bins` equal-width buckets over `[min low, max high]` and returns the centres of the buckets whose volume is at least `node_frac` times the largest; it returns nothing for no candles, `bins < 1`, `hi <= lo` or a non-positive peak (P01-S059 to P01-S063). INTERPRETATION: nodes are bucket centres, not traded prices; adjacent nodes are not merged; a series of zero volumes (for example volumes defaulted to 0 by `from_kite`) gives no nodes (P01-S062, P01-S063, P01-S099). A NaN or wrong-side infinite price raises `ValueError` or `OverflowError` here, uncaught (P01-S064). FACT: `volume_bins` and `volume_node_frac` are not in the committed file, so the loader defaults 24 and 0.7 apply (P01-S065); the one non-test caller passes the daily series when one is present, otherwise the first series in the dict (P01-S066; the choice is the zone builder's, section 4).

**Confluence scoring (`confluence.py`).**

- FACT: `ConfluenceContext` carries the prior-day levels, the volume nodes, `now` and `recency_window_days` (90.0); `ScoringParams` carries six weights, `t_high` 5.0, `t_med` 3.0, `touch_cap` 4, `merge_pct` 0.4, `band_buffer_pct` and `recency_min_factor`; neither validates its values (P01-S067, P01-S068). The only non-test constructions are in the zone builder (P01-S069). At the four layers (dataclass default, builder fallback, loader default, committed file) every value agrees except `band_buffer_pct`: 0.0 in the dataclass, 0.1 in the builder, the loader and the committed file (L516); `touch_cap`, `recency_min_factor` and `recency_window_days` are not in the committed file (P01-S069). INTERPRETATION: the dataclass defaults apply only where `ScoringParams` is built directly, as the unit tests do (P01-S069).
- FACT: `score_zones` merges each kind across the timeframes and returns every group, RESISTANCE then SUPPORT, each in ascending centre; nothing is filtered or sorted by score (P01-S070). `_merge_across_tfs` sorts the zones of one kind from all timeframes by centre and joins a zone to the current group when its centre is within `merge_pct` per cent of the group's running mean centre (P01-S071). INTERPRETATION: the tolerance compares centres, not band edges; the mean drifts upward as members join; and zones of one timeframe can join one group, so SR-C1's "ACROSS timeframes" describes the purpose rather than a rule; the merged band is the union, gaps included (P01-S072 to P01-S074).
- FACT: `_score_group` appends up to six votes in a fixed order: `swing_pivots` (`w_swing × min(touches, touch_cap)`), `multi_tf` (`w_multi_tf ×` the number of distinct timeframe labels), `volume_node` and `prior_day_level` (each when a level falls in the band widened by `band_buffer_pct`), `round_number` (no buffer) and `recency`; the score is their sum and the bucket is HIGH when `score >= t_high`, at least two distinct method types and at least two timeframes, MEDIUM when `score >= t_med`, else LOW (P01-S075, P01-S076, P01-S080). The formula is written out in P01-S077; with the three committed timeframes a group scores between 2 and 10 (INTERPRETATION) (P01-S077).
- INTERPRETATION (the committed weights): `swing_pivots` and `multi_tf` are always appended, so the "≥2 distinct method-types" condition is always met; every group present on all three timeframes is HIGH, a two-timeframe group is HIGH from three summed touches, a single-timeframe group can never be HIGH, and a lone pivot is LOW (or MEDIUM only with a round number and a full recency factor) — all without any volume, prior-day, round-number or recency vote (P01-S078, P01-S079, P01-S121). The volume, prior-day and round-number votes are recorded and counted as method types even at weight 0 (latent at the committed weights) (P01-S081). The score is compared unrounded; `to_dict` rounds it for storage (P01-S082).
- FACT and INTERPRETATION (the two band tests): `_level_in_band` uses the same inclusive, centre-relative buffer as `contains` (P01-S083). `_has_round_number` tests whether the band contains a multiple of the power of ten below its centre or of half of it; INTERPRETATION: the first step adds nothing, so the rule is "a multiple of half the power of ten below the centre", whose step jumps tenfold at each power of ten (multiples of 5 for a centre of 99.9, of 50 for 100.1), between about 5 and 50 per cent of price (P01-S084, P01-S085). A NaN or `+inf` centre raises `ValueError` or `OverflowError`, uncaught (P01-S086). A second, related rule (`round_number_levels`) is in the zone builder, for the anchor payload (P01-S087).
- FACT: `_recency_factor` returns 0.0 for no touch time, 1.0 when `now` is `None` or the window is non-positive, strips both time zones when exactly one side is naive, and otherwise returns `1 − age_days / window`, clamped to at most 1.0 and at least `recency_min_factor` (P01-S088). INTERPRETATION: the decay is linear in calendar days (weekends and holidays included); a missing clock gives every dated zone the full recency vote; the stripped comparison gives the elapsed time only when both wall clocks are IST; a `recency_min_factor` above 1 would escape the upper clamp (P01-S089 to P01-S091).
- INTERPRETATION: the four files are deterministic functions of their inputs; output depends on input order only through the pivot positions (P01-S092). FACT: zones come out by ascending centre, not by score or distance; the detector logs the first twelve of each kind in that order (loader default `max_zones_logged` 12), so with more zones the lowest-priced are kept (the slicing is the detector's logic, section 5); the consumers that choose a zone by price use their own key (P01-S093).

**Data quality across the four files (summary; A05 CM-05, CM-10, HA-04, HA-08).**

- INTERPRETATION: nothing from `from_kite` to `score_zones` orders candles by time; only the pivots depend on order (P01-S094). A duplicated bar can add touches and doubles its volume in the profile; gaps are not detected; nothing de-duplicates by `ts` (P01-S095). A forming last bar cannot be a pivot but can decide its neighbours and adds partial volume (P01-S096). Bad values: `from_kite` raises on missing or non-numeric prices and accepts NaN and infinity; the pivots silently skip NaN windows; the volume profile and the round-number test raise on NaN or infinities; mixed naive and aware times raise in two `max` calls but not in the recency factor; none of the four files checks `high >= low`, positive prices or non-negative volume, and none catches an exception (P01-S097). Zero and negative prices make the percentage tolerances zero or negative (P01-S098). A series of fewer than 11 bars at `n = 5` gives no pivots (P01-S100). Every tolerance is a percentage of a price; none uses ATR, a tick size or an absolute amount; the pivot window counts bars, the recency window calendar days (P01-S101). Whether any such value reached the code in production is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (P01-U02, P01-U03).

## 4. Candle fetch, zone building, the zone cache and the zone warmer

This section covers `sr_detector/fetch.py` (`OhlcFetcher`), `sr_detector/zone_builder.py` (the knobs, the parameter builders, prior-day levels, the zone-building path, round numbers, intraday anchors and the two payloads), `sr_detector/zone_cache.py` (`ZoneSet`, `ZoneCache`) and `sr_detector/zone_warmer.py` (`ZoneWarmer`). **The four files.**

- FACT: `fetch.py` 159 lines, `zone_builder.py` 224, `zone_cache.py` 76, `zone_warmer.py` 161 (P02-S007). None reads the system clock directly: every time value comes from an injected `now_fn` or a `now` argument, in every trading-process construction `time_authority.now_ist` (the wall clock in IST) (P02-S009, P02-S093). `fetch.py` imports nothing from `broker/` and receives the broker call as an argument (SR-F1) (P02-S008, P02-S011).
- HISTORICAL: `fetch.py` declares "Detector-side fetch orchestration: resolve a symbol → instrument_token, fetch the 3 timeframe windows (one request each), cache within the session, and FAIL SAFE (never raise; an error yields no candles so the detector logs a fetch_failed row and skips analysis — spec C)", with SR-F1 to SR-F4; `zone_builder.py` "The SINGLE zone-building path, shared by the V1 detector (async observer) and the V2 ZoneWarmer" (P02-S010, P02-S142, P02-S147).

**`OhlcFetcher`: the injected closure, the methods and the window.**

- FACT: every non-test construction passes the one closure `_sr_fetch_fn`, built once at `main.py` L3479 to L3487 when any of six switches is on (`_v1_on`, `_v2_on`, `_struct_exit_on`, `_regime_on`, `_v3_chain_on`, `_watchlist_on`); the class calls it positionally, once per cache miss, never while holding its lock, and never retries (P02-S012 to P02-S014).
- FACT: `fetch_timeframes` resolves the token (`None` gives `{}`) and fetches each interval with the constructor's lookback, adding an interval only when its result is non-empty; `fetch_interval` fetches one interval with a lookback of its own; `fetch_by_token` returns `None` for a falsy token without a log line and keys the cache by `cache_key` or `__token_<n>` (P02-S015, P02-S017, P02-S018). INTERPRETATION: a failed interval and an empty one are both simply absent, so a caller cannot tell from the result which intervals failed (P02-S016).
- FACT: `_resolve_token` reads `instrument_token` from the instrument cache and returns `token or None`, logging a WARNING only on an exception (P02-S020). INTERPRETATION: a missing symbol, a row without the attribute and a `None` token are logged and give no candles; a token of 0 gives no candles silently (A05 IN-02) (P02-S021).
- FACT and INTERPRETATION: the window runs from `now − lb` calendar days to `now`, with `now` the injected clock at the moment of the miss; no market hours, session or trading calendar is used (P02-S022, P02-S023). INTERPRETATION and UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED: the fetcher applies no as-of truncation and no completeness test, so a still-forming last candle, if the broker sent one, is converted, cached and returned as received; whether it did is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A05 HA-04) (P02-S024).
- FACT: rows are converted with `Candle.from_kite` in the order the closure returned them, inside the `try`, so one unconvertible row discards the interval (P02-S025; P01-S019). No line of the four files sorts candles, removes duplicates, detects gaps, checks that timestamps increase or checks values (A05 CM-05) (P02-S026). An empty or `None` answer is returned as `None`, not cached and not logged (P02-S027).

**The fetcher's cache.**

- FACT: the key is `(symbol or cache key, interval)` with no lookback or window, and the value is the injected clock's reading and the list (P02-S028). The age is the difference of two `now_fn` readings, the wall clock in IST, while the field comment L49 says "fetched_at_monotonic_dt" (P02-S029, P02-S144). The test is `age > ttl`, so an entry exactly as old as the TTL is served; the PB-01 and one-minute fetchers are built with a TTL of 0 (P02-S031). Nothing is ever evicted, and there is no size limit (P02-S032). A hit returns the stored list object itself (P02-S033). One `threading.Lock` is held only for the dictionary read and write (P02-S034).
- INTERPRETATION: a backward clock step makes an entry live longer than its TTL and a forward step expires it early; ages run through nights and holidays (P02-S030). A TTL of 0 still stores every list and serves it only at a zero or negative age (P02-S031). Every caller of one object within the TTL receives the same mutable list (P02-S033). Two threads that miss on one key both fetch; there is no single-flight (P02-S034).
- INTERPRETATION (A05 HA-06; note N2): because the key has no window, a second request for one key with a different window inside the TTL receives the first request's list; no committed construction makes such a pair; with `intraday_anchors_enabled: true` and `anchor_intraday_interval: 30minute` (a value the loader accepts) a failed structural `30minute` fetch followed by a successful one-day anchor fetch would give later structural requests one day of bars as their 180-day series, with no log line; on the export script's per-call fetcher, whose clock is constant, the anchor request receives the structural list when the command's interval is a structure interval (P02-S042, P02-S160). Whether either occurred is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (P02-S042).

**Failures, logging, retries and the limiter (A04 RL-05, A05 HA-01).**

- FACT: one `except Exception` covers the clock read, the window, the closure (the limiter and the broker call), every row's conversion and `_cache_put` (P02-S035). The file writes two log lines only, WARNING "token lookup failed" and WARNING "historical_data failed", the second for every exception, including a limiter timeout or shutdown abort in which no broker call was made, a broker or library error (a 429 among them), a conversion failure and a cache-write failure; there is no 429 path, no `penalize` and no counter (P02-S037, P02-S038, P02-S104, P02-S107). The shutdown abort is logged at WARNING while its class's docstring says "INFO (not WARN/ERROR)" (P02-S107).
- INTERPRETATION: two paths raise to the caller despite "never raise": a clock failure in `_cache_get` (outside the `try`) and a token that `int` cannot convert in `fetch_by_token`; a `_cache_put` failure after a successful broker call is reported as "historical_data failed" and the candles are discarded (P02-S019, P02-S036). FACT: the four files contain no retry, backoff or sleep on the fetch path; the only pacing is the closure's `acquire("historical")` (A04's), and a failed interval is asked again only by the next miss (P02-S039).

**The seven constructions (P02-S040; committed values only).**

- FACT: (1) the V1 detector's fetcher, built inside `build_sr_detector` (`sr_detector/__init__.py` L53 to L60), 180 days, TTL 1800 s, thread "sr-detector", under `sr_detector.enabled` (committed `true`); (2) the market regime's (A18), under `regime.enabled` (`false`); (3) the structure fetcher for the zone warmer, 180 days, TTL 1800 s, thread "sr-zone-warmer", under `_v2_on or _struct_exit_on` (both `false`); (4) the V3 chain's (A18), under `v3_chain_mode != "off"` (committed `"shadow"`); (5) the PB-01 watchlist's (A18), one object shared by three A18 stages, TTL 0, under `watchlist.enabled` (`true`); (6) the retest monitor's one-minute fetcher, two days, TTL 0, thread "sr-retest-monitor", inside the V2 block; (7) the export script's, one per analysis, in its own process over its own closure and limiter, with a constant naive clock (P02-S040). INTERPRETATION: at the committed values the trading process would build rows 1, 4 and 5, and only row 1's consumer is A08's; each object has its own cache; they share only the closure and, through it, the limiter's `historical` bucket and the client; which objects existed in production is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (P02-S041).
- FACT: `effect_telemetry.register_constructed("ohlc_fetchers")` follows the five constructions in `main.py`; the V1 detector's fetcher, built inside the package, has no such call (A05 XC-03's fetcher-count point; P02-S099).

**`zone_builder`: knobs, parameters and the one zone-building path.**

- FACT: `_attr` is `getattr(cfg, name, default)`, so a fallback applies only where the object lacks the attribute (P02-S043). `ZoneKnobs` (seven fields, no defaults), `build_zone_knobs`, `build_scoring_params` (twelve fields, cast; the one builder fallback that differs from the dataclass default is `band_buffer_pct`, 0.1 against 0.0) and `build_flag_params` (`entry_proximity_pct` 1.0, `volume_surge_mult` 1.5, `weak_breakout_frac` 0.25, `retest_sl_buffer_pct` 0.3) read the configuration object; the values at each layer are in P02-S045 and P01-S069 (P02-S044 to P02-S047).
- FACT: `prior_day_levels` returns the high, low and close of `daily[-2]` (or `daily[-1]` for a one-candle list), by list position only (P02-S048). INTERPRETATION: that is the previous trading day only when the series ends with a candle for today, and otherwise the day before; a descending or duplicated series gives other candles; whether the broker included today's candle is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (P02-S048).
- FACT: `scored_zones_from_candles` runs, for each interval, `find_swing_pivots` with `pivot_n_by_tf.get(tf, default_pivot_n)` and `cluster_zones`, then builds the `ConfluenceContext` from the daily series (or the first series) and calls `score_zones`; every non-test production of scored zones (the V1 detector, the warmer, the V3 runner, the PB-01 runner and the watchlist capture) goes through it (P02-S049, P02-S051). INTERPRETATION: with no daily series the "prior-day levels" and the volume nodes come from the first intraday series; an empty dictionary raises `StopIteration`, which no non-test caller passes (P02-S050). FACT: `split_zones` splits by kind, keeping the input order (P02-S052).
- FACT and INTERPRETATION: `round_number_levels` lists the multiples of the power of ten below a price and of its half, below and above, rounded to six decimals, and returns `()` for a missing or non-positive price; a NaN or infinite price raises (P02-S053, P02-S054). `intraday_anchors` keeps the fine candles whose label's wall-clock date is the candidate's date and whose wall-clock time is in `[session_open, session_close)`, computes A05's `session_vwap` on a non-empty scope and the opening-range high and low over the first `orb_window_minutes` (P02-S055). INTERPRETATION: the scoping fits labels that mark the start of a bar in IST wall-clock time; there is no completeness rule, so a call during the opening window or with a forming bar uses partial data (P02-S056 to P02-S058). FACT: `build_anchor_payload` and `build_swings_payload` build the two evidence payloads; INTERPRETATION: without a daily series the anchor payload's `prior_day` is empty while the confluence context uses the first series; the swing payload lists the merged group's values under each contributing role (P02-S059 to P02-S062).

**`ZoneCache`.**

The one construction (`main.py` L3557 to L3558) passes `sr_detector.zone_cache_ttl_sec` (committed 1800) and `now_ist`; one `RLock` guards the dictionary (P02-S064, P02-S071). `put` replaces the symbol's entry with `warmed_at` = the time of the `put`; `get` returns `None` for no entry or an age above the TTL (P02-S065, P02-S066). `symbols_due_for_rewarm` returns every entry whose age is at least `ttl − margin`, with no upper bound (P02-S067). `evict_expired`, `size` and `clear` have no non-test caller, and nothing clears the store during the object's life; the end-of-day square-off clears the retest monitor's parked candidates, not the zone cache (P02-S068, P02-S073).

**`ZoneWarmer`.**

- FACT: the one construction (`main.py` L3564 to L3568, inside `if _v2_on or _struct_exit_on:` and a `try` that sets the warmer and the cache to `None` on failure) passes no `idle_sleep_sec`, `max_queue` or `enabled`, so 2.0 s, 512 and `True` apply; no configuration key reaches the queue size, the idle sleep or the enable flag, and `sr_detector.max_queue` (loader 256) feeds only the V1 detector's own queue (A02 K-23) (P02-S074 to P02-S076, P02-S102). `start` returns without a log line when disabled or already started and otherwise starts the daemon thread "sr-zone-warmer"; it is called at `main.py` L3796 (V2 block) or L3816 (structure-exit block) (P02-S077, P02-S078).
- FACT (A06 WD-10, PQ-11; note N3): `enqueue` returns at once when disabled or for a falsy symbol; it deduplicates under `_pending_lock`, then calls `put_nowait`; on a full queue it drops the symbol with a WARNING and removes it from `_pending`; any other error is logged at ERROR; it makes no fetch, clock read, store call or cache call (P02-S079, P02-S080). INTERPRETATION: the dispatcher's `_warm_zones` call never waits for a fetch, the limiter, the broker or a free queue slot; it can wait only for two short locks and, on the drop and error paths, the logger (P02-S080, P02-S111, P02-S130). How long those waits took is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (P02-S112).
- FACT and INTERPRETATION: `enqueue` has two callers, A06's `_warm_zones` on a signal's arrival and the warmer's own re-warm scan; neither reader of the zone cache enqueues, so a symbol has a `ZoneSet` only after a signal for it has reached the dispatcher in the current process (P02-S159). The dedup covers only queued symbols, so a symbol can be queued again while it is warmed (P02-S081).
- FACT: the worker takes one symbol at a time with a 2.0-s timeout and, when the queue is empty, runs `_enqueue_rewarms`; `warm` fetches the knobs' intervals, logs INFO "fetch miss" and stores nothing on an empty result, and otherwise scores, splits and `put`s, logging INFO "warmed" (P02-S082, P02-S084, P02-S085). INTERPRETATION: a fetch miss keeps the old entry, which stays due; any non-empty result is stored, even with only some intervals or no zone at all (returning `True`, against the docstring "Returns True if zones were cached"); the re-warm scan returns expired entries too, so every symbol warmed once is re-warmed about every 25 minutes (TTL 1800 s minus margin 300 s; committed values) for the life of the process, after the close included, and a symbol whose re-warm gets no interval at all is queued again at every idle scan, about every 2 s (P02-S083, P02-S084, P02-S086).
- FACT and INTERPRETATION: `stop` sets the stop event, posts a sentinel, joins for 2.0 s and clears the worker reference whether or not the join completed (called at `main.py` L1744); a worker inside a broker call is not interrupted and can still write to the cache after the join returns; `enqueue` keeps accepting symbols after `stop` (P02-S088, P02-S089). At the committed values (`wait_for_retest_enabled` and `structure_exit_enabled` both `false`) no warmer, zone cache or structure fetcher is built, and the processor's `_warm_zones` is a no-op (P02-S090, P02-S100).
