## 2. `strategies/control.py`: the strategy-control resolver (Q2, Q3)

### 2.1 Declared purpose

- P02-S008 HISTORICAL: the module docstring (L1 to L27, last changed in `8116b74`, 2026-07-10) calls the module the "Slice 2 strategy-control resolver (single source of truth)"; `strategy_will_trade()` is "the SOLE authority for the question 'will this strategy trade today?'", imported by the entry gate (`signals/signal_processor`) and the status table (`scripts/cron_officer` / pre-flight), "so the gate and the displayed table can NEVER disagree" (L4 to L7).

### 2.4 Evaluation order and outcomes

- P02-S017 FACT: the checks run in this order and the first one that matches returns (L79 to L117):

| Order | Lines | Condition | `will_trade` | `reason` (verbatim start) | `product` | `cause` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | L81 to L83 | `not enabled` | False | `WON'T TRADE — switch disabled` | `intent` | `DISABLED` |
| 2 | L90 to L97 | `force_intraday_only and intent == "DELIVERY"` | False | `WON'T TRADE — emergency breaker (force_intraday_only) forces intraday; delivery strategy dormant` | `"INTRADAY"` | `FORCE_BREAKER` |
| 3 | L100 to L106 | `trade_type == "INTRADAY" and intent != "INTRADAY"` | False | `WON'T TRADE — master INTRADAY blocks this DELIVERY strategy` | `intent` | `TRADE_TYPE` |
| 4 | L107 to L113 | `trade_type == "DELIVERY" and intent != "DELIVERY"` | False | `WON'T TRADE — master DELIVERY blocks this INTRADAY strategy` | `intent` | `TRADE_TYPE` |
| 5 | L116 to L117 | none of the above | True | `WILL TRADE — enabled, master allows {intent}` | `intent` | `OK` |

- P02-S018 FACT: the comparisons are exact, case-sensitive string tests (`==`, `!=`) with no normalisation of `trade_type` or `intent` (L90, L100, L107); `force_intraday_only` is used by truthiness (L90).
- P02-S019 FACT (precedence): a disabled strategy always gets `DISABLED`, whatever `trade_type` and the breaker say (order 1). A DELIVERY strategy with the breaker on gets `FORCE_BREAKER`, also when `trade_type` is INTRADAY, which alone would give `TRADE_TYPE` (order 2 before order 3). An INTRADAY strategy is never stopped by the breaker branch (L90 tests `intent == "DELIVERY"` only); with the breaker on and `trade_type` DELIVERY it gets `TRADE_TYPE` (order 4).
- P02-S020 FACT: a `trade_type` value other than `"INTRADAY"` or `"DELIVERY"` fails both tests at L100 and L107 and, for an enabled strategy not stopped by the breaker, reaches the WILL TRADE return; comment L114: "trade_type == "BOTH", or a matching intent → permitted". INTERPRETATION: the resolver treats any out-of-set `trade_type` (for example a lower-case `"intraday"`, `None` or a typo) exactly like `"BOTH"`; the validation of the value is left to the caller's source (section 4).

HISTORICAL: the `Verdict` docstring L53 to L57 says `product` is "the EFFECTIVE (post-force-rewrite) product the gate's placement path uses" and that the status table "sources the strategy's TRUE declared intent separately"; comment L99 speaks of the "(effective) intent".
