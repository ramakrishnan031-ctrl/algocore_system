## 2. The file, its declared contract and its intent sources

- P06-S007 HISTORICAL (module docstring L1 to L31): the purpose is to "Hold screened signals in a price-watchlist. Poll LTP at fixed interval", release to the `on_release` callback when price "retraces into entry_price +/- tolerance zone", and also release "on hard timeout (EG6) or 3 consecutive quote failures (EG9)" (L4 to L8). Fifteen "Locked Design Decisions" EG1 to EG15 follow (L10 to L25), among them EG7 "Release protocol: remove -> state_store -> log -> on_release" (L17), EG10 "_watchlist guarded by Lock, snapshot outside lock" (L20), EG11 "add() raises on dup; remove() and _release() are no-ops" (L21), EG14 "Integration with signal_processor deferred to Module 33" (L24) and EG15 "NOT in scope: wiring, non-price strategies, priority ordering" (L25). "What This Module Does NOT Do" includes "Does not wire into signal_processor (Module 33's job)" (L30).
- P06-S008 FACT: the legacy module instruction file for module 33 is `docs/web_claude/01_module_instructions/module_33_startup_checks.txt`; a search of it for `entry_gate`, `EntryGate`, `pullback`, `continue_from_gate` and `on_gate_release` finds no match. INTERPRETATION: the module that EG14 names as the integrator does not mention the gate in its instruction file.
- P06-S009 HISTORICAL (`docs/web_claude/01_module_instructions/module_31_entry_gate.txt`, the gate's design instruction; P10 owns the legacy documents, this part cites only the gate's own design file): the context paragraph L5 to L9: "After a signal PASSES screening, if the strategy has pullback_wait_enabled=True, the signal is held in a watchlist until price retraces to the entry level (or timeout).
