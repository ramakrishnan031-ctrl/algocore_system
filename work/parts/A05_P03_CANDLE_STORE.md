## 2. The candle record (`CandleData`) and the accumulator (`_Accumulator`)

P03-S009. HISTORICAL (`data/candle_store.py` L1 to L10): the module docstring calls the file a "Minute OHLC candle builder (LF9-LF18)" that "Builds minute candles from LTP ticks" and is the "Single source of truth for intraday candle data"; its "Audit 3.4 fixes" are "Candle OHLC built from LTP only (never from tick["ohlc"])" and "Candle close triggered by clock thread, not by tick arrival".

P03-S010. FACT (L12 to L21): the imports are `from __future__ import annotations`, the standard modules `threading`, `time`, `collections.deque`, `dataclasses.dataclass`, `datetime.datetime` and `timedelta`, `typing`, and `now_ist` from `core.time_authority`. No broker library, database, configuration, event-bus or rounding module is imported.

P03-S011. FACT (L24 to L36): `CandleData` is a `@dataclass(frozen=True)` with the fields `instrument_token: int`, `symbol: str`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close` (each `float`), `volume: int`, `ts: datetime`, `interval_sec: int` and `is_synthetic: bool = False`. HISTORICAL (field comments): `volume` "FIX-135 Item 46: accumulated from ticks (was always 0)" (L33); `ts` "IST candle close time (from clock thread)" (L34); `is_synthetic` "FIX-020: True for flat carry-forward candles" (L36); class docstring "LF13: Immutable candle record emitted on candle close" (L26).

P03-S012. INTERPRETATION (Python dataclass semantics): `frozen=True` makes assignment to a field of an emitted candle raise `FrozenInstanceError`; the dataclass checks no types and no values at construction, so the annotations are not enforced.

P03-S013. FACT (L39 to L48): `_Accumulator` is a mutable `@dataclass` with `open`, `high`, `low`, `close` (floats), `tick_count: int`, `window_start: datetime` (comment "FIX-049: minute boundary for this accumulator", L47) and `volume: int = 0` (comment "FIX-135 Item 46: accumulated volume from ticks", L48).

P03-S014. FACT (L50 to L63): `_Accumulator.update(ltp, volume=0)` sets `high = ltp` when `ltp > high` (L57 to L58), `low = ltp` when `ltp < low` (L59 to L60), `close = ltp` (L61), adds 1 to `tick_count` (L62) and adds the `volume` argument to `volume` (L63). `open` is never changed after the accumulator is created. HISTORICAL (docstring L51 to L56): the FIX-049 `exchange_timestamp` late-tick discard "was removed (it was dead — never wired — and a landmine ...). Every tick routed here is accepted; window assignment is clock-driven upstream."

P03-S015. INTERPRETATION (code reading of L50 to L63 and L187 to L195) (B3): for one token and one interval, open is the first LTP routed to the store after the previous close (or after the last `mark_reconnect`), high and low are the maximum and minimum of the routed LTPs, close is the last routed LTP, and volume is the sum of the `volume` arguments. "First" and "last" are in the order of the `on_tick` calls, not in exchange-time order. The store treats the `volume` argument as a per-tick increment: it adds whatever value it is given and computes no difference between successive values.

P03-S016. FACT (grep of `window_start` and `\btick_count\b` in non-test Python: only `data/candle_store.py` L46, L47, L62, L188, L193, L194): `window_start` and `tick_count` are written but never read; neither is copied into `CandleData` (L316 to L328).

P03-S017. FACT (grep of `round\(|Decimal|tick_size|math\.|isnan|isfinite` in `data/candle_store.py`: no match; L50 to L63, L314 to L347) (B7, I5): the store does no rounding, no tick-size snapping and no precision handling, and checks no price for sign, zero or finiteness. Prices are compared and copied as the floats it is given; a synthetic candle copies the previous candle's close unchanged (L287, L338 to L341). Tick-size inputs are not used by the store. Upstream, the published library divides the packet integer by the segment divisor (part P02, P02-S048) and the feed applies `float()` (part P01, P01-S041).

P03-S018. FACT and INTERPRETATION (I5; `orders/smart_tgt_manager.py` L57; A04 AUDIT, CG-27): tick-size rounding of prices derived from candles happens only downstream of the store, for example in the smart-TGT trail, which imports `_round_down_to_tick` and `_round_up_to_tick` from `broker/slippage_engine.py` (L57); those helpers are A04's (CG-27) and their use is A11's. The candle helpers of `core/candle_math.py` are part P04's subject; A08's use of candles stays A08's.

## 3. `CandleStore` construction, lifecycle, token map and `gc_sweep`

P03-S019. FACT (L66 to L104): `CandleStore.__init__(logger, candle_interval_sec=60)` stores the logger and the interval (L84 to L85) and creates one `threading.Lock` (L88; comment L87, HISTORICAL: "LF17: single lock guards _accum, _history, _token_map, _on_close_cbs"), the empty `_accum` dict (token to accumulator, L89), `_history` dict (token to deque of candles, L90), `_on_close_cbs` list (L91), `_token_map` dict (L92), `_last_closed_window = None` (L97), `_consecutive_synthetic_only = 0` (L101), `_stop_event` (a `threading.Event`, L103) and `_timer_thread = None` (L104). HISTORICAL (class docstring L67 to L73): "LF9: Build minute OHLC candles from LTP stream ... A clock thread fires every candle_interval_sec and closes all active accumulators, emitting CandleData to registered callbacks."

P03-S020. FACT (L75 to L77, L358): the history length is not a constructor parameter: `MAX_HISTORY = 500` is a class constant, used as the `maxlen` of each token's deque. HISTORICAL (comment L75 to L76): "LF14: generous buffer beyond 1 full trading day (390 = 6.5h * 60m). 500 accommodates multi-inning trail calculations without unbounded growth."

P03-S021. FACT (L110 to L118): `start()` clears `_stop_event`, creates a `threading.Thread(target=self._timer_loop, daemon=True, name="candle-store-timer")`, stores it in `_timer_thread` and starts it. There is no guard against a second call. INTERPRETATION (Python semantics): a second `start()` while a timer runs would start a second timer loop, and both would close candles at every boundary. FACT (grep of `candle_store\.start\(` in non-test Python): the only call is `main.py` L3922.

P03-S022. FACT (L120 to L122, L249 to L250): `stop()` only sets `_stop_event`; it does not join the thread and does not close the open accumulators. When the event is set, `_stop_event.wait(...)` returns True and the loop breaks without calling `_close_candles()`. INTERPRETATION: the candles being built when `stop()` is called are never emitted, stored or passed to any callback. The timer thread is a daemon thread, so if it is still running at interpreter exit it is abandoned (Python semantics).

P03-S023. FACT (grep of `_timer_thread|candle-store-timer` in non-test Python: only `data/candle_store.py` L104, L113, L116, L118): no code checks whether the timer thread is alive or restarts it; `main.py` calls only `start()` (L3922) and `stop()` (L1792).

P03-S024. FACT (L128 to L131): `set_token_map(token_map)` replaces `_token_map` with `dict(token_map)` under the lock. HISTORICAL (docstring L129): "LF15: Inject instrument_token -> symbol mapping."

P03-S025. FACT (`main.py` L3945 to L3946; `core/instrument_cache.py` L287 to L293; grep of `token_map\(|set_token_map` in non-test Python): the store's map is set once, at boot, with `instrument_cache.token_map()`, which returns `{r.instrument_token: r.symbol for r in self._rows}`, one entry for every row of the instrument cache (a later row with the same token overwrites an earlier one; Python dict semantics). No other non-test code calls the store's `set_token_map`. INTERPRETATION (D5, candle-store side): the store keeps the boot-time copy for the whole process; nothing refreshes it, and it covers every cached instrument, not only subscribed ones. What `symbol` holds and how the cache is built are part P05's subject.

P03-S026. FACT (L133 to L168): `gc_sweep(valid_tokens)` takes the lock, deletes every `_accum` entry and every `_history` entry whose token is not in `valid_tokens` (L149 to L160), counts each removed token once (L159), logs INFO "candle_store.gc_sweep: removed N dead tokens" when N > 0 (L162 to L166) and returns N. It does not change `_token_map` or the callback list. HISTORICAL (docstring L134 to L145): "FIX-092: Garbage-collect dead tokens from internal state ... Called on InstrumentsRefreshed event to clean up delisted symbols."

P03-S027. FACT (greps of `gc_sweep`, `InstrumentsRefreshed` and `\.reload\(` in non-test Python; `core/events.py` L222 to L234; `core/instrument_cache.py` L188 to L215) (A01 C29 (f), candle-store side): no non-test code calls `gc_sweep` (the only matches are its definition, L133, and its own log text, L164); nothing subscribes to `InstrumentsRefreshed` (the only matches are its class, `core/events.py` L223, and its publication inside `InstrumentCache.reload`, `core/instrument_cache.py` L206 to L214); `reload` has no non-test caller; and the event carries only `token_count` (`core/events.py` L234), while `gc_sweep` needs the set of valid tokens.

P03-S028. INTERPRETATION (code reading of L133 to L168, L189 to L195, L353 to L359): in the wired system no path removes a token's history. Each token's history is bounded by `MAX_HISTORY`, and the set of tokens with history can only grow during a process. If `gc_sweep` were called, a token removed from `_history` but still in `_token_map` would get no synthetic candles until it had a real candle again (synthetic candidates need history, L283 to L287).
