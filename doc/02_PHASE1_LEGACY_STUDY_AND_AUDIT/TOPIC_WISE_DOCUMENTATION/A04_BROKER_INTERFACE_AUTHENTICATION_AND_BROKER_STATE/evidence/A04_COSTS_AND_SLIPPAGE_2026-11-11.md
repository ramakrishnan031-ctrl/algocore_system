## 3. `CostCalculator.calculate_cost`: validation, formulas, units and rounding

Signature (L112 to L121): `calculate_cost(self, side: str, qty: int, price: float, product: str, exchange: str = "NSE", is_fno: bool = False, fno_kind: str = "FUTURES") -> CostBreakdown`.

### 3.1 Validation and rejected inputs

- **P10-S021** FACT: `side` other than `"BUY"` or `"SELL"` raises `ValueError` (L145 to L146); there is no case conversion, so `"buy"` is rejected.
- **P10-S022** FACT: `product` other than `"MIS"`, `"CO"` or `"CNC"` raises `ValueError` (L147 to L148). The check does not depend on `is_fno`, so `"NRML"` (and any other code, for example `"BO"`) is rejected for cash and F&O alike.
- **P10-S023** FACT: `exchange` other than `"NSE"` raises `ValueError` ("Only 'NSE' is currently supported (BSE deferred to v2.1).", L149 to L153).
- **P10-S024** FACT: The FIX-112 check (L154 to L159, `6da0e6b`, 2026-05-18) raises `ValueError` when `is_fno and exchange != "NSE"`. INTERPRETATION (control flow): it can never fire, because every `exchange` other than `"NSE"` has already raised at L149 to L153; the check is unreachable.
- **P10-S025** FACT: With `is_fno=True`, `fno_kind` other than `"FUTURES"` or `"OPTIONS"` raises `ValueError` (L160 to L163). With `is_fno=False`, `fno_kind` is neither validated nor used.
- **P10-S026** FACT: `qty` and `price` are not validated. The docstring says "qty: number of shares (must be > 0)" and "price: execution price per share (must be > 0)" (L127 to L128), and lists `ValueError` only for "unknown side, product, exchange, or fno_kind" (L142 to L143); no code checks the sign, type or size of `qty` or `price`.
- **P10-S027** INTERPRETATION (Python `decimal` semantics; `_d` converts with `str()`, L84): a `None` or a non-numeric value for `qty` or `price` makes `Decimal(str(value))` raise `decimal.InvalidOperation`, not `ValueError`; a zero `qty` or `price` gives a zero turnover and every component `0.00`; a negative turnover gives a negative proportional brokerage, which `min()` selects over the positive flat cap, so brokerage (where it is charged) and every percentage component come out negative. No caller-facing error distinguishes these cases.

### 3.2 Turnover and the components

- **P10-S028** FACT: `turnover_d = _d(qty) * _d(price)` (L166). It is not rounded; it is returned as `float(turnover_d)` (L241).
- **P10-S029** FACT (brokerage, L168 to L174): for `product` `"MIS"` or `"CO"` (both sides) and for `"CNC"` on a `"SELL"`: `proportional = brokerage_pct_intraday / 100 × turnover` and `brokerage = _r2(min(brokerage_flat_intraday, proportional))`; for `"CNC"` on a `"BUY"`: `brokerage = 0.00`. This is the locked CC3 (`docs/locked_decisions.yaml` L1181 to L1191, which states "CNC BUY: 0 (delivery buy is free at Zerodha)").
- **P10-S030** FACT: Brokerage does not depend on `is_fno`: an F&O leg uses the same two equity fields `brokerage_flat_intraday` and `brokerage_pct_intraday`. One call charges at most one flat cap; the method has no notion of several executed orders, partial fills or slices within a leg (one `qty`, one `price`).
- **P10-S031** FACT (STT, L176 to L198): with `is_fno` and `"FUTURES"`: `futures.stt_pct / 100 × turnover` on both sides (L178 to L180); with `is_fno` and `"OPTIONS"`: on a `"SELL"` `options_sell.stt_pct / 100 × turnover` (L182 to L184), on a `"BUY"` `options_buy.stt_pct / 100 × turnover` (L185 to L187); a third F&O branch returns `0.00` (L188 to L189). Without `is_fno`: `"MIS"`/`"CO"` `"SELL"` `stt_sell_pct / 100 × turnover`, `"BUY"` `0.00` (L190 to L195); `"CNC"` `stt_cnc_pct / 100 × turnover` on both sides (L196 to L198). INTERPRETATION (control flow): the third F&O branch (L188 to L189) is unreachable because of the `fno_kind` check at L160 to L163.
- **P10-S032** FACT: For options the docstring says "Options STT is on premium, futures on notional" (L136 to L137); the code always uses `turnover = qty × price`. INTERPRETATION: the premium or the notional basis depends entirely on what the caller passes as `price` and `qty`; there is no lot-size input, so a lot multiplier, if any, would be the caller's. No non-test caller passes `is_fno` or `fno_kind` (grep `is_fno\s*=|fno_kind\s*=` over non-test code: outside `broker/cost_calculator.py`, whose own hits are the docstring line L136, the error message L157, the comparisons L178, L181, L213 and L220 and the round-trip methods passing on their own parameters, L268, L285 and L289, and outside `docs/` (nine lines in `docs/web_claude/`), the only hits are `broker/slippage_engine.py` docstring L16 to L17, `core/instrument_cache.py` L148, `orders/mis_autosquareoff.py` L22 and `scripts/refresh_instruments.py` L187, none a call of the calculator), so the F&O branches are reached only from test code (`tests/unit/test_cost_calculator.py`, 7 lines with `is_fno=True`; A19).
- **P10-S033** FACT (exchange transaction charge, L200 to L201): `exchange_txn = _r2(exchange_txn_pct / 100 × turnover)`, on both sides, for every product, and also for F&O legs (the same field; the comment at `config/broker_costs.yaml` L18 calls it the "NSE exchange transaction charge").
- **P10-S034** FACT (SEBI fee, L203 to L204): `sebi = _r2(sebi_pct / 100 × turnover)`, both sides, every product.
- **P10-S035** FACT (GST, L206 to L209): `gst = _r2(gst_pct / 100 × (brokerage_d + exchange_txn_d + sebi_d))`, where the three terms are the already-rounded components. STT and stamp duty are not in the GST base. This is the locked CC6 (`docs/locked_decisions.yaml` L1215 to L1221: "Applied to the rounded component values (CC10)").
- **P10-S036** FACT (stamp duty, L211 to L228): on a `"SELL"`: with `is_fno` and `"OPTIONS"`, `options_sell.stamp_duty_pct / 100 × turnover` (L213 to L215), otherwise `0.00` (L216 to L217); on a `"BUY"`: with `is_fno`, `futures.stamp_duty_buy_pct` (FUTURES) or `options_buy.stamp_duty_pct` (OPTIONS) `/ 100 × turnover` (L218 to L223); without `is_fno`, `stamp_duty_mis_buy_pct` for `"MIS"`/`"CO"` (L224 to L225) and `stamp_duty_cnc_buy_pct` for `"CNC"` (L226 to L228). INTERPRETATION: the section comment "Stamp duty — BUY side only" (L211) holds at the file values only because `options_sell.stamp_duty_pct` is `0.0` (`config/broker_costs.yaml` L35); a non-zero value there would charge stamp duty on an options sell.

## 4. The round trip: `round_trip_breakdown` and `total_round_trip_cost`

- **P10-S042** FACT: `round_trip_breakdown(self, qty, entry_price, exit_price, product, exchange="NSE", is_fno=False, fno_kind="FUTURES") -> CostBreakdown` (L272 to L300; docstring "Combined CostBreakdown for BUY entry + SELL exit (v14)", L282) calls `calculate_cost("BUY", qty, entry_price, ...)` (L283 to L286) and `calculate_cost("SELL", qty, exit_price, ...)` (L287 to L290), and returns a `CostBreakdown` whose every field is `round(buy.<field> + sell.<field>, 2)` (L291 to L300).
- **P10-S043** FACT: `total_round_trip_cost(...)` (L244 to L270), with the same parameters, returns `self.round_trip_breakdown(...).total` (L266 to L270). Its docstring says "Total cost of a complete trade: BUY entry + SELL exit (CC12). Used by the paper engine for realistic P&L accounting (Project Rule 15)" (L255 to L257) and "Returns: Sum of calculate_cost("BUY", ...).total + calculate_cost("SELL", ...).total" (L263 to L264).
- **P10-S044** FACT: Neither method has a direction parameter. The entry leg is always costed as a `"BUY"` at `entry_price` and the exit leg as a `"SELL"` at `exit_price`, with one `qty` for both legs. The locked CC12 states this convention (`docs/locked_decisions.yaml` L1271 to L1276: "Calls calculate_cost("BUY", qty, entry_price, product) + calculate_cost("SELL", qty, exit_price, product)"); it does not mention SHORT trades.
- **P10-S045** INTERPRETATION (from P10-S031, P10-S036 and P10-S044): for a SHORT trade (entry by a SELL, exit by a BUY), the sell-side STT of MIS/CO is computed on the exit turnover and the buy-side stamp duty on the entry turnover, the reverse of the sides actually traded; for CNC, STT is on both sides, so only the stamp duty and the CNC-BUY brokerage exemption move. Worked example (independent recomputation, P10-S009): MIS, quantity 100, SHORT entry at 510.00 and exit at 500.00: costed as called (BUY at 510.00, SELL at 500.00) 53.44; with each leg costed on the side actually traded (SELL at 510.00, BUY at 500.00) 53.66; difference 0.22. Whether SHORT trades reached these paths is A07's and A10's and runtime (section 16).
- **P10-S046** FACT: The round-trip fields are rounded with the built-in `round(x, 2)` on `float` sums (L292 to L299), a different function from the per-leg `Decimal` `ROUND_HALF_UP` (L87 to L89). INTERPRETATION (float semantics): each leg's six components and its total are already 2-decimal values, so the sum of two of them is a 2-decimal value up to binary representation error, and `round(x, 2)` only removes that error; for those seven fields the different rounding rule cannot change the amount (turnover, which a leg does not round, is the exception: P10-S048).
- **P10-S047** FACT: The round trip's `total` is `round(buy.total + sell.total, 2)` (L298), computed from the two leg totals, not from the six round-trip components. INTERPRETATION: it equals the sum of the six round-trip components up to float representation, because each leg total is the exact sum of its rounded components (P10-S037).
- **P10-S048** FACT: The round trip's `turnover` is `round(buy.turnover + sell.turnover, 2)` (L299): it is rounded to 2 decimal places, while a single leg's `turnover` is not rounded (L241).
- **P10-S049** FACT (non-test callers, grep): `round_trip_breakdown` is called by `orders/order_placer.py` L2347 (section 7.3) and, inside the module, by `total_round_trip_cost` (L266); `total_round_trip_cost` only by `round_trip_costs_or_zero` (L340); `calculate_cost` only by `round_trip_breakdown` (L283, L287). No non-test code calls `calculate_cost` for a single leg. The docstring's "Used by the paper engine" (L257) names no existing caller (P10-S012).

- **P10-S109** FACT (`apply`, L94 to L138): `side` is upper-cased (L106) and anything other than `"BUY"` or `"SELL"` raises `ValueError` (L107 to L108); `price <= 0` is returned unchanged (L109 to L110, comment "MARKET-with-no-LTP path; nothing to slip."); otherwise `tier = self.tier_for(symbol)` (L112), `bps = self._cfg.tiers[tier].slippage_bps` (L113), `delta = price * (bps / 10_000.0)` (L114), and `adjusted = price + delta` for a BUY or `price - delta` for a SELL (L115): always against the trader (SE3, L22 to L24).
- **P10-S110** FACT (tick handling, L117 to L138): `tick = self._tick_size(symbol)`, which is `get_by_symbol(symbol).tick_size` or `None` on `InstrumentNotFoundError` (L140 to L144). With no tick or a non-positive tick the adjusted price is returned unrounded (L118, L138). With `abs(delta) < tick / 2` (FIX-050 sub-tick guard, L119 to L130, `35877bc`) it is returned unrounded, with a DEBUG line only when a logger was given (L124 to L129). Otherwise a BUY is rounded up and a SELL down to the tick (`_round_up_to_tick`, `_round_down_to_tick`, L132 to L137), away from the trader.
- **P10-S111** FACT (units): `slippage_bps` is in basis points, 1 bps = 0.01 % of price (SE5 L27; the header of `config/slippage_model.yaml` L4; the schema comment `core/config_loader.py` L2266); the adjustment is computed in `float` (L114 to L115) and the tick rounding in `Decimal` through the helpers (section 9).
- **P10-S112** INTERPRETATION (independent recomputation, P10-S009; tick 0.05): a BUY at 500.00 with 15 bps gives 500.75 and a SELL 499.25; a BUY at 123.45 with 5 bps gives raw 123.511725, rounded up to 123.55 (about 8.1 bps in effect), and a SELL 123.35; a BUY at 20.00 with 5 bps has delta 0.01, below half a tick, and returns 20.01 unrounded, which is not a multiple of 0.05; a BUY at 5.00 with 15 bps returns 5.0075 (the docstring's own example, L121 to L122). So the slippage applied ranges from the configured bps up to the configured bps plus almost one tick, and the sub-tick guard returns prices that are not tick multiples.
- **P10-S113** FACT: The design notes and the code differ in two places. SE4 (L25 to L26, `9841029`): "Result is rounded to instrument tick_size when known. SE6 (L28 to L29): `apply()` makes "No mutation, no I/O, no logging from the hot path"; the code can log at DEBUG from `apply()` when a logger is given (L124 to L129), though the `main.py` instance has none (P10-S100).
