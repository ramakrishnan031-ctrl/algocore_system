### 4.3 Threads and lifecycle

- **P06-S058** FACT: `start()` (L503 to L514) returns if the thread is alive, else clears the stop event and starts one daemon thread named "order-monitor" running `_poll_loop`. `_poll_loop` (L594 to L600) waits `poll_interval_sec` on the stop event before each cycle, so the first cycle runs one interval after `start()`, and it has no exception handler of its own (an exception escaping `_poll_cycle` would end the thread; the per-order work inside `_poll_cycle` is wrapped, L623 to L630, while the `_check_force_close` call L615 is not). `stop()` (L516 to L521) sets the event and joins for at most 5 s (literal). `is_alive()` (L523 to L528) is True only while the thread runs.
- **P06-S059** FACT (non-test call sites): `main.py` calls `rehydrate_from_store(store)` (L3952) and then `start()` (L3960) at boot, after the synchronous `reconcile_once()` (L3880) and the stale-order sweep (L3891); `cancel_all_entry_orders()` (L1768) and then `stop()` (L1774) in `_shutdown`; and `is_alive` from the `/health` liveness function (L4060 to L4067). - **P06-S060** FACT (threads that run the monitor's code): (1) the poll thread, which runs `_poll_cycle` and, by synchronous dispatch, the subscribers of the events it publishes (section 8.3); (2) the callers' threads, which run `track()` with its possible `get_quote` call (order placer, EOD square-off, structure exit manager) and `untrack()`; (3) the main thread, which runs `rehydrate_from_store` before `start()`, and `cancel_all_entry_orders()` while the poll thread is still running (stopped one step later, `main.py` L1767 to L1776); (4) the thread of the health endpoint that calls `is_alive()` (A15).
- **P06-S061** FACT (shared state): `_watched` and `_internal_to_composite` are accessed under `self._lock` (L289 to L307, L425 to L427, L473 to L475, L486 to L490, L494 to L496, L500 to L501, L546 to L547, L604 to L605, L620 to L622); the `_WatchEntry` objects, the two failure counters and `_force_close_fired_date` are read and written without a lock (L641, L667, L693 to L703, L708 to L766, L812 to L818, L895 to L906); each cycle iterates a shallow copy of `_watched` taken under the lock (L604 to L605, OM10). INTERPRETATION: the counters and the force-close date are written only by the poll thread; a watch entry can be read by the main thread's shutdown cancel while the poll thread updates it; the lock is not held while the bus publishes, so subscribers that call `track()` or `untrack()` on the poll thread do not deadlock on it.

## 5 The poll cycle, the broker read and the failure counters

### 5.1 One cycle

- **P06-S062** FACT: `_poll_cycle` (L602 to L630) copies `_watched` under the lock; builds one `_OrphanTickCache` for the cycle (L612); calls `_check_force_close(snapshot)` (L615; section 7.5); then, for each entry, skips it if its key has left `_watched` since the copy (L620 to L622) and calls `_process_order(entry, tick_cache)` inside `try/except Exception`, which logs `order_monitor.process_order_failed` at ERROR with the traceback (L623 to L630) and goes on to the next entry.
- **P06-S063** FACT: the cycle does not test the stop event between entries (L618 to L630): after a failure branch sets the stop event (section 5.3), the remaining entries of the same cycle are still processed, each with its own broker call; the loop ends at the next `while` test (L596).

### 5.2 The broker read and what the monitor takes from it

- **P06-S064** FACT: `_process_order` (L632 to L790) calls `adapter.get_order_history(entry.broker_order_id)` (L639). ### 6.2 OPEN

- **P06-S089** FACT: `_handle_open` (L877 to L881) calls `_safe_transition(id, "OPEN")`, then `_check_fill_timeout` (section 7.1), then `_check_price_movement_cancel` (section 7.2), in that order and unconditionally: the second check runs even when the first has just cancelled and untracked the order (no test of a return value or of `is_watching` between them). - **P06-S092** FACT: `_handle_complete` (L981 to L1020) computes `final_qty` (the polled quantity, or `entry.qty` when that is 0) and `final_price` (the polled average, or `entry.expected_price` when that is 0) (L989 to L990); calls `_safe_transition(id, "COMPLETE")` and returns at once, without untracking, when it returns False (L992 to L994); computes the slippage from `final_price` and `entry.expected_price` (L996); publishes `OrderFilled` with `final_qty`, `final_price`, `expected_price`, `slippage_pct` and `filled_at=now_ist()` (L997 to L1012, not inside a `try`); logs INFO `order_monitor.complete`; and untracks (L1020).
- **P06-S093** FACT: `final_qty` and `final_price` are locals; `entry.filled_qty` and `entry.avg_fill_price` are not updated before the transition, so the `OrderStatusChanged(status="COMPLETE")` that `_safe_transition` publishes first is built from the entry (L1321 to L1326): quantity 0 and price `None` for an order whose fill was not seen as PARTIAL, or the last partial figures otherwise. This is the A04 side of A03 R-04 (section 16).
- **P06-S094** FACT: when the broker reports COMPLETE with a zero quantity or a zero average price, `OrderFilled` carries the requested quantity or the expected price in their place (L989 to L990); `filled_at` is the monitor's poll time, not a broker time (L997, L1010). Whether the broker returns such rows is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED.
- **P06-S095** FACT: the slippage formula (`_calc_slippage_pct` L136 to L148) is, for BUY, `(avg_fill - expected) / expected * 100` and, for any other side, `(expected - avg_fill) / expected * 100`, with 0.0 when `expected == 0.0`; it is OM9 of the build instruction (module_14 L72 to L78, HISTORICAL). INTERPRETATION: an entry rehydrated from a MARKET row has `expected_price` 0.0 (section 10.2), so its slippage is reported as 0.0.

### 6.5 CANCELLED and REJECTED

- **P06-S096** FACT: `_handle_terminal(entry, osm_state)` (L1022 to L1070), called with "CANCELLED" (L780, L930, L968) and "FAILED" (L783): when `entry.filled_qty > 0` it first publishes `OrderPartiallyTerminated(..., filled_qty, avg_fill_price, expected_price, slippage_pct, filled_at=now_ist(), reason=osm_state)` (L1033 to L1051, not inside a `try`) and logs WARNING `order_monitor.partial_terminated`; then it transitions, which publishes `OrderStatusChanged` (L1064), and untracks when the transition returns, True or False (L1065 to L1070); when the refusal is re-raised (P06-S118) the entry stays watched.
- **P06-S097** FACT: for a REJECTED order the `OrderStatusChanged(status="FAILED")` carries `rejection_reason=None` (P06-S065), so the broker's rejection text does not leave the monitor.
- **P06-S313** FACT: for a broker CANCELLED or REJECTED row the monitor passes neither the row's `filled_qty` nor its `avg_price` to `_handle_terminal` (L779 to L783), although it reads both from every last row (L764 to L765); only the PARTIAL and COMPLETE branches pass them on (L774, L777). INTERPRETATION: for an ENTRY leg the order placer then takes its zero-fill branch, releasing the reservation and marking the trade FAILED (`orders/order_placer.py` L1963 to L2015, A10). Whether the broker reports a partial fill on OPEN-set rows, or a positive filled quantity on a CANCELLED or REJECTED row, is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (P06-S276).

- **P06-S098** FACT: the monitor never requests EXPIRED, UNKNOWN_IN_FLIGHT, SUBMITTED or PENDING through `_safe_transition` (its call sites L564, L840, L879, L908, L992, L1064, L1113, L1123, L1198 request OPEN, PARTIAL, COMPLETE, CANCELLED or FAILED); it moves an order to SUBMITTED only inside the PENDING-to-OPEN auto-step (L1273) and on rehydration (L448). ## 8 `_safe_transition`, the events and the persistence boundary

### 8.1 What `_safe_transition` does with each request

- **P06-S123** FACT: built only after a committed transition (L1305 to L1342): `source_module="order_monitor"`, `internal_order_id`, `broker_order_id` (the entry's; "" when `entry` is None, which no call site passes, L1312 to L1320), `status` = the requested state, `qty_filled` = `entry.filled_qty`, `avg_fill_price` = `entry.avg_fill_price` when above 0.0 else `None`, `rejection_reason` = `entry.status_message` when the state is FAILED or CANCELLED and the message is not empty, else `None`. ## 9 Empty history and orphan detection

- **P06-S137** FACT: an empty list from `get_order_history` (L705 to L758) first resets the failure counters (L691 to L703, as any successful read); then `entry.empty_history_count += 1`; below 3 (a literal, L709) nothing else happens; at 3 or more the cycle's `_OrphanTickCache` (or a fresh one when `tick_cache` is None, L716 to L718) supplies the set of broker order ids from `adapter.get_open_orders()`, fetched at most once per cycle and only when a candidate needs it (L117 to L133).
- **P06-S138** FACT: three outcomes: the fetch failed (set is None): WARNING `empty_history_orphan_unverified` and `_fire_orphan` (L720 to L733); the id is in the set: INFO `empty_history_false_positive` and the count reset to 0 (L734 to L745); otherwise WARNING `empty_history_orphan_confirmed` and `_fire_orphan` (L746 to L757). A non-empty history resets the count (L760).
- **P06-S139** FACT: `_fire_orphan` (L871 to L875) calls the orphan callback and untracks; it makes no OSM transition and publishes no event, so the OSM keeps the order's last state and `orders.status` its last written value. INTERPRETATION: the wired callback soft-kills and alerts (`main.py` L825 to L843); the trade's capital and status are left to their owners (A10, A12, A13).
- **P06-S140** FACT: in Live `get_open_orders()` (`broker/zerodha_adapter.py` L1898 to L1923) takes a "margins" token ("reuse quota bucket", L1899), calls `kite.orders()` and keeps only orders whose upper-cased status is "OPEN" or "TRIGGER PENDING" (L1908, L1922); a raised exception is translated under the operation name `get_margins` (L1903). - **P06-S141** FACT: the second source treats as absent every order whose order-book status is neither "OPEN" nor "TRIGGER PENDING": the transitional statuses of P06-S086 (PUT ORDER REQ RECEIVED, VALIDATION PENDING, OPEN PENDING and others) and the final ones (COMPLETE, CANCELLED, REJECTED). INTERPRETATION: an order whose history read keeps returning empty while its order-book status is one of those is reported as a confirmed orphan.
- **P06-S142** FACT: the empty-history count is per entry (L708), kept across cycles, with no time window; an exception branch returns before it (L654, L662, L689) and leaves it unchanged.
- **P06-S143** HISTORICAL: the H-15 comments (L710 to L715, L721 to L724; `27e40da`, 2026-04-19) give the design: an order still reported open means "a transient broker-side anomaly"; an unverifiable one is fired "to match pre-H-15 behaviour. ## 10 Restart behaviour

### 10.1 What a restart loses

- **P06-S145** FACT: the OSM dictionary, the watch list, both failure counters, the force-close date and every entry's `partial_since`, `empty_history_count`, `tgt_price` and `sl_price` live only in memory (`broker/order_state_machine.py` L153 to L155; `broker/order_monitor.py` L216 to L236, L57 to L77); the entry's `filled_qty` and `avg_fill_price` also reach `orders.qty_filled` and `orders.avg_fill_price` through `OrderStatusChanged` (`broker/order_monitor.py` L1323 to L1326; `orders/order_manager.py` L159 to L160, L741 to L743), but the rehydration read does not select those columns (`core/state_store.py` L1059 to L1070), so a rehydrated entry starts at 0 and 0.0 (`broker/order_monitor.py` L463 to L472, with the defaults at L67 and L68). The original internal ids are not persisted (`orders/order_manager.py` L306: "internal_order_id is NOT stored — kept in memory by order_placer").

### 10.2 Rehydration of the watch list

- **P06-S146** FACT: `rehydrate_from_store` (L386 to L482) runs: (1) `_cleanup_orphaned_pending_trades(state_store)` (L408; section 10.3); (2) `state_store.get_open_orders_for_rehydration()` (L411), with an ERROR log and a return of 0 on an exception (L412 to L416); (3) for each row with a non-empty `order_id`: skip it if already rehydrated (L424 to L427); register the broker order id itself as the OSM id (the "synthetic internal" id, L423, L432 to L441; `ValueError` ignored, any other exception logged as WARNING and the row still processed); if the stored status, upper-cased, is OPEN, PARTIAL, TRIGGER_PENDING or SUBMITTED, try one OSM transition to it and ignore any exception (L443 to L450); parse `placed_at` with `datetime.fromisoformat`, falling back to `now_ist()` (L452 to L456); key the entry by broker id, symbol and the date of `placed_at` (L458 to L461); build the entry from the row (`side` from `transaction_type`, `qty` from `qty_requested`, `expected_price` from `price` or 0.0, `leg` from `leg`; no `tgt_price` or `sl_price`) and store it (L463 to L475); (4) log the count at INFO (L478 to L481).
- **P06-S147** FACT: the store read (`core/state_store.py` L1041 to L1078) returns `orders` rows joined to `trades` with `o.status IN ('PENDING', 'SUBMITTED', 'OPEN', 'PARTIAL', 'TRIGGER_PENDING')`, ordered by `placed_at`, with no date filter and no condition on the trade's status (A03 P-27). It omits UNKNOWN_IN_FLIGHT and the space form 'TRIGGER PENDING', both allowed by the CHECK (A03 P-06).
- **P06-S148** FACT: against the OSM table, from PENDING only SUBMITTED is allowed (`broker/order_state_machine.py` L101): "OPEN" and "PARTIAL" are refused and "TRIGGER_PENDING" is not an OSM state, and the refusal is swallowed (L449 to L450). A row stored as OPEN, PARTIAL or TRIGGER_PENDING, or as PENDING (for which no move is tried), therefore starts in OSM PENDING; only a row stored as SUBMITTED starts in OSM SUBMITTED. The comment L443 to L444 (`7ed3a3b`, 2026-04-22) gives the purpose: "so the first poll's transition does not go PENDING → COMPLETE (illegal)".
- **P06-S149** INTERPRETATION (from P06-S116 and P06-S118): for an entry in OSM PENDING, the first poll succeeds through the auto-step when the broker reports a status of the OPEN set, and through PENDING to FAILED for REJECTED; when the broker reports COMPLETE, CANCELLED or PARTIAL, the refusal is re-raised on every cycle: no `OrderFilled`, no untrack, an ERROR with traceback each cycle and, for CANCELLED after a seen partial fill, a repeated `OrderPartiallyTerminated` (P06-S122). Rows written from the monitor's events hold OPEN or PARTIAL while the order lives (P06-S135) and the inserts write PENDING (`orders/order_manager.py` L324, L377), so the case arises for an order that filled or was cancelled while the process was down. ### 11.2 Competing vocabularies and sets

- **P06-S171** FACT: three vocabularies meet here: (a) the broker's strings, which the adapter passes through unchanged in `get_order_history`, `get_open_orders` and `get_all_orders` (`broker/zerodha_adapter.py` L1205, L1913, L1977), with spaces, and which the monitor and the open-orders filter upper-case before comparing (L763; L1922); (b) the nine OSM states, which the schema comment names as the `orders.status` write vocabulary (`core/schema.sql` L335 to L342: "Kite 'REJECTED' maps to FAILED in order_monitor and no raw broker strings reach this column"); (c) extra strings accepted at the storage boundary or by consumers: 'TRIGGER_PENDING' and 'TRIGGER PENDING' (schema, store filter, the monitor's rehydration tuple) and REJECTED (the event docstring and the placer's sets).

### 11.3 Lossy mappings

- **P06-S175** FACT: the broker status string itself is neither stored nor published by the monitor: `OrderStatusChanged.status` is the requested OSM state (L1338), and the history entry is discarded after the cycle.
- **P06-S176** FACT: OPEN, TRIGGER PENDING and SUBMITTED all become OPEN (P06-S088).
- **P06-S177** FACT: FAILED stands for three causes that the stored status does not separate: a broker REJECTED (L783), a cancel that did not succeed after a fill timeout (L1123), and, in the OSM only, any exception of the placement call, timeouts included (adapter L658; P06-S048); the field that could separate them, `rejection_reason`, is always `None` from the monitor (P06-S065).
- **P06-S178** FACT: partial-fill information is lost in three ways: a status of the OPEN set with a positive filled quantity records no fill (P06-S085), a cancel finalises with the entry's stored figures, set only by a PARTIAL poll (P06-S106), and the fill figures of a CANCELLED or REJECTED row are not used (P06-S313).
- **P06-S179** FACT: the final fill quantity and price of a COMPLETE order reach `OrderFilled` but not `OrderStatusChanged` (P06-S093; A03 R-04).
- **P06-S180** FACT: broker times are replaced by local times: `OrderHistoryEntry.ts` is the adapter's clock (P06-S064) and `filled_at` is the monitor's or the writer's clock (L997; `orders/order_manager.py` L155).
- **P06-S181** FACT: statuses outside the seven mapped strings produce no state and no record (P06-S083), and the empty-history orphan outcome changes neither the OSM nor `orders.status` (P06-S139).

### 11.4 Summary for question 16

- **P06-S182** FACT (summary, for the AUDIT to classify): within the monitor the mapping from broker strings to OSM states is fixed for seven strings (OPEN, TRIGGER PENDING and SUBMITTED to OPEN; PARTIAL; COMPLETE; CANCELLED; REJECTED to FAILED) and silent for every other string; EXPIRED and UNKNOWN_IN_FLIGHT exist in the OSM but no non-test code enters them. The broker's actual status vocabulary at the time is not recorded in the repository beyond one HISTORICAL capture (P06-S086).
