### 4.2 Lifecycle and threading

- P03-S030 FACT (`start`, L110 to L116): returns at once if `not self._enabled` or a worker is already set; otherwise clears the stop event, creates `threading.Thread(target=self._run, name="sr-detector", daemon=True)`, starts it and logs at INFO "sr_detector: worker started (mode=%s, version=%s)" with `_mode` and `_version`.

- P03-S032 FACT (the worker loop `_run`, L144 to L158): `while not self._stop.is_set()`: `item = self._q.get(timeout=0.25)`, `continue` on `queue.Empty`; then, inside `try`: skip the sentinel (L151 to L152) or call `self.process_candidate(item)` (L153); `except Exception as exc` logs at ERROR "sr_detector: worker error for %s: %s" with `getattr(item, "symbol", "?")` (L154 to L156); `finally: self._q.task_done()` (L157 to L158).
- P03-S033 INTERPRETATION (what `stop` leaves; from P03-S031, P03-S032 and P03-S005 (a), (b)): (a) a candidate being processed when `stop` is called is finished (fetches, analysis and write), because the loop tests the stop event only between items; (b) candidates still in the queue are not processed: after the current item the loop condition fails, and when the worker was idle in `get` the next item it takes is normally the sentinel; they are dropped without a log line or a row; (c) `join` waits at most 2.0 s; a worker still inside a candidate's fetches is left running, unreported, and `_worker` is set to `None` anyway; as a daemon thread it is stopped abruptly when the interpreter exits ("Their resources (such as open files, database transactions, etc.) may not be released properly"); (d) a `start()` after a timed-out `stop()` would pass the `_worker is not None` test and start a second worker beside the first; at the baseline no caller does this (`main.py` starts once, L3504, and stops once, L1697).

### 4.3 `observe`

- P03-S037 FACT (`observe`, L131 to L140; docstring "Enqueue a placed candidate and return immediately. Never raises."): `if not self._enabled: return`; then `try: self._q.put_nowait(candidate)`; `except queue.Full:` WARNING "sr_detector: queue full, dropping %s" with `candidate.symbol` (L137 to L138); `except Exception as exc:` ERROR "sr_detector: observe() failed: %s" (L139 to L140, comment "belt-and-suspenders; the pipeline must not see this"). - P03-S038 INTERPRETATION (non-blocking; P03-S005 (a)): `put_nowait` either places the candidate at once or raises `Full`; it never waits for a free slot, and it does not wait for the analysis. A full queue (256 candidates waiting at the loader default) drops the new candidate: no row is ever written for it, and the WARNING line is the only record.
- P03-S039 INTERPRETATION (what runs on the caller's thread): the `_enabled` test, one `put_nowait` (which takes the queue's internal lock briefly, P03-S005 (a): "implements all the required locking semantics") and, only on a full queue or an unexpected exception, one log call with the logger's handler work. Nothing else of the observer runs on the caller's thread.
- P03-S040 INTERPRETATION (the one unguarded expression): in the `queue.Full` handler the argument `candidate.symbol` (L138) is evaluated before `_safe_log` is entered, outside any further `try`; an object without a `symbol` attribute would raise `AttributeError` out of `observe`. - P03-S041 FACT and INTERPRETATION (behaviour when disabled): with `_enabled` false, `start` creates no thread (L111) and `observe` returns at once (L133 to L134); `process_candidate` and `analyze` still work when called directly (they do not test the flag). INTERPRETATION: `main.py` builds the detector only when `enabled` is true (L3491), so in the trading process `_enabled` is always true when a detector exists and the off state is "no detector" (`sr_detector=None`; `_sr_observe` returns at `signals/signal_processor.py` L637 to L638); the export script forces `enabled: True` in its copy (`scripts/sr_level_export.py` L152 to L155) and never calls `start` or `observe`. ### 5.2 `compute_flags_and_retest`: nearest zones and distances

- P03-S062 FACT (the opening steps, L55 to L76): `entry = float(candidate.intended_entry)` (L61); `resistances` and `supports` are the zones whose `kind` is `"RESISTANCE"` or `"SUPPORT"` (L62 to L63); `nearest_res = _nearest(resistances, entry, prefer_above=True)` and `nearest_sup = _nearest(supports, entry, prefer_above=False)` (L65 to L66); the distances (L68 to L69, P03-S065); `flags = []` and `retest = RetestProposal()` (L71 to L72); if `scored_zones` is empty, the flag `"NO_CLEAR_STRUCTURE"` is appended and the result returned at once (L74 to L76).
- P03-S063 FACT (`_nearest`, L114 to L122): `None` for an empty list; with `prefer_above=True` the preferred pool is the zones with `band_high >= entry`, otherwise the zones with `band_low <= entry`; `pool = preferred or zones`; returns `min(pool, key=lambda z: abs(z.center - entry))`.
- P03-S064 INTERPRETATION (what "nearest" means; from P03-S063, P01-S004 (b), P01-S093): the nearest resistance is the resistance zone whose centre is closest to the entry among those reaching up to or above the entry (a zone that contains the entry qualifies); only when no resistance reaches the entry does it fall back to all resistances, so the recorded "nearest resistance" can lie wholly below the entry. The mirror holds for supports (the fallback can give a support wholly above the entry). The choice ignores confidence (LOW zones included) and distance limits; on equal distances `min` keeps the first zone in list order, which is ascending centre.
- P03-S065 FACT and INTERPRETATION (the distances, L68 to L69): `dist_res = ((nearest_res.center - entry) / entry * 100.0)` and `dist_sup = ((entry - nearest_sup.center) / entry * 100.0)`, each `None` when the zone is `None` or `entry` is falsy (0.0). INTERPRETATION: both are percentages of the entry price, measured to the zone's centre, not to its nearer edge; they are positive when the resistance is above and the support below the entry, and negative in the fallback cases of P03-S064. The column comments (`core/schema.sql` L1271 to L1272) state no unit or sign.

### 5.3 The "into" flags

- P03-S066 FACT (the two branches, L78 to L102): if `candidate.is_long` (P01-S031: true only for `LONG` or `BUY` after strip and upper-case): `overhead = nearest_res`; if it is not `None` and `_is_into_zone(entry, overhead, params.entry_proximity_pct, from_below=True)`: append `"BUYING_INTO_RESISTANCE"` and set `retest = _retest_for_long(overhead, params)` when `overhead.confidence == "HIGH"`, else append `"LOW_CONFIDENCE_STRUCTURE"` (L80 to L86); then `_breakout_flags(flags, _broken_zone(resistances, entry, is_long=True), breakout, params, is_long=True)` (L90 to L91). Otherwise (every other direction value): the mirror with `underlying = nearest_sup`, `from_below=False`, `"SELLING_INTO_SUPPORT"` and `_retest_for_short` (L94 to L100), then `_breakout_flags(flags, _broken_zone(supports, entry, is_long=False), breakout, params, is_long=False)` (L101 to L102).
- P03-S067 FACT (`_is_into_zone`, L138 to L146; docstring "Long: entry sits at/inside the band or just below it (within proximity), but NOT above band_high (that is a breakout, handled separately). Short mirrors."): `pad = zone.center * (proximity_pct / 100.0)`; long: `(zone.band_low - pad) <= entry <= zone.band_high`; short: `zone.band_low <= entry <= (zone.band_high + pad)`.
- P03-S068 INTERPRETATION (the "into" rule; from P03-S066, P03-S067): a long entry is "into" resistance when it lies inside the nearest resistance's band or below its bottom by at most `entry_proximity_pct` per cent of the band's centre (1 per cent committed, so 5.0 for a zone centred at 500), both ends inclusive; an entry above the band's top is never "into" it. Only the one nearest resistance is tested: another HIGH zone that also satisfies the test is not looked at. The pad is one-sided (below for a long, above for a short), unlike `ScoredZone.contains`, which widens both sides (P01-S024).
- P03-S069 FACT and INTERPRETATION (the confidence rule; from L82 to L86 and L96 to L100): when the entry is "into" the nearest zone, the flag is `BUYING_INTO_RESISTANCE` (or `SELLING_INTO_SUPPORT`) only for a zone whose `confidence` is exactly `"HIGH"`; for `"MEDIUM"` or `"LOW"` it is `LOW_CONFIDENCE_STRUCTURE`. INTERPRETATION: `LOW_CONFIDENCE_STRUCTURE` therefore means "the entry is into the nearest zone, and that zone is not HIGH"; when the entry is into no zone, no confidence flag is raised, whatever the confidences are.

### 5.4 The breakout flags

- P03-S070 FACT (`_broken_zone`, L125 to L135; docstring "The zone a breakout is breaking THROUGH: for a long, the resistance just below entry (greatest band_high <= entry); for a short, the support just above entry (smallest band_low >= entry). None if there is no such zone."): long: among the zones with `band_high <= entry`, the one with the greatest `band_high`; short: among the zones with `band_low >= entry`, the one with the smallest `band_low`; `None` if there is none. It was added by `55c93d3ca0baff8b007b6a9a6f2f0cb603fdb362` (2026-06-27; section 11.4).
- P03-S071 FACT (`_breakout_flags`, L149 to L175): returns if the zone or the breakout context is `None` (L157 to L158); `width = max(zone.band_high - zone.band_low, 1e-9)` (L159); long: `cleared = last_close > band_high`, `poke_fail = last_high > band_high and last_close <= band_high`, `weak = cleared and (last_close - band_high) < weak_breakout_frac * width` (L161 to L165); short: `cleared = last_close < band_low`, `poke_fail = last_low < band_low and last_close >= band_low`, `weak = cleared and (band_low - last_close) < weak_breakout_frac * width` (L166 to L170); `any_breakout = cleared or poke_fail`; append `"WEAK_BREAKOUT"` if `weak or poke_fail` (L172 to L173); append `"NO_VOLUME_CONFIRMATION"` if `any_breakout and avg_volume > 0 and last_volume < volume_surge_mult * avg_volume` (L174 to L175).
- P03-S072 INTERPRETATION (what the breakout rules test; from P03-S070, P03-S071, P03-S053): (a) the "broken" zone is chosen against the entry, any confidence, at any distance below it (a long's broken resistance can lie far below the entry); (b) `cleared` compares the breakout candle's close (the last 30-minute candle, P03-S053) with that zone's top, and nothing requires that candle, or any recent candle, to have crossed the zone: for a long whose last close is above the nearest lower resistance, a "breakout" is counted even if the price has been above that zone for weeks; (c) `WEAK_BREAKOUT` is a close above the top by less than a quarter of the zone's width (loader `weak_breakout_frac` 0.25), or a candle whose high passed the top while its close did not; for a zone of zero width the floor `1e-9` leaves only the poke-and-fail case (P01-AP04 records zero-width zones); (d) `NO_VOLUME_CONFIRMATION` is a breakout of either kind with the candle's volume below 1.5 times the average of the preceding candles; it is never raised when the average is 0 (for example when every volume defaulted to 0 in `Candle.from_kite`, P01-S018), and a breakout candle still forming carries only its volume so far (P03-S053); (e) both flags can be raised together; (f) no breakout flag is possible when the fetch returned none of `30minute`, `60minute` and `day` (configured timeframes `15minute` and `5minute` only), because the breakout context is then `None` (P03-S052).
- P03-S073 FACT (order and de-duplication, L104 to L107): `seen` and `ordered = [f for f in flags if not (f in seen or seen.add(f))]` keep the first occurrence of each flag in order; the result's `flags` is `tuple(ordered)`. The possible order is: the "into" flag (`BUYING_INTO_RESISTANCE`, `SELLING_INTO_SUPPORT` or `LOW_CONFIDENCE_STRUCTURE`), then `WEAK_BREAKOUT`, then `NO_VOLUME_CONFIRMATION`; `NO_CLEAR_STRUCTURE` only alone (L74 to L76).
- P03-S074 INTERPRETATION (the combinations; from P03-S066 to P03-S073): each call appends each flag at most once (one "into" flag at most; each breakout flag at most once), so the de-duplication changes nothing at the baseline. The "into" zone and the "broken" zone are normally different zones (the first reaches up to or above the entry, the second lies at or below it); they are the same zone only when the entry equals that zone's top exactly (`band_high == entry` meets both `>=` and `<=`), and then a long can carry `BUYING_INTO_RESISTANCE` together with the breakout flags of that same zone. A candidate with zones but no flag gets an empty tuple and `structure_status` `OK` (P03-S047).

### 5.5 The V1 retest proposal

- P03-S075 FACT (`_retest_for_long`, L178 to L181; comment "Wait for a break above and a retest of the zone from above (res→support)"): `sl = zone.band_low * (1.0 - params.retest_sl_buffer_pct / 100.0)`; returns `RetestProposal(would_wait=True, entry=zone.band_high, sl=sl)`. `_retest_for_short` (L184 to L186): `sl = zone.band_high * (1.0 + params.retest_sl_buffer_pct / 100.0)`; returns `RetestProposal(would_wait=True, entry=zone.band_low, sl=sl)`. `RetestProposal` is `sr_detector/models.py` L156 to L161 (`would_wait=False`, `entry=None`, `sl=None` by default; docstring "V1 logs the PROPOSAL only — the hypothetical outcome is a later EOD step"; P01-S033).
- P03-S076 INTERPRETATION (what the proposal is; from P03-S066, P03-S075, P03-S081): a proposal exists only when `BUYING_INTO_RESISTANCE` or `SELLING_INTO_SUPPORT` is flagged, that is, for an entry into a HIGH zone; for a long it proposes an entry at the zone's top (a retest after a break above) and a stop `retest_sl_buffer_pct` per cent (loader 0.3; the key is not in the committed file) below the zone's bottom; for a short the mirror. It is written to the row's `would_wait_for_retest` (1 or 0), `proposed_retest_entry` and `proposed_retest_sl` (P03-S081) and is used by nothing else: no non-test code outside `flags.py` and `detector.py` names `RetestProposal`, `compute_flags_and_retest` or the proposal's fields, and no code reads the three proposal columns back (`git grep` of the names and of the three column names, with the four exclusions, outside `tests/` and `docs/`: only `flags.py`, `detector.py`, `models.py`, the schema, the store's column tuple and the V2 diverter's own write). No code simulates the proposal: the column `hypothetical_retest_result` is never written (P03-S090).
- P03-S077 FACT and INTERPRETATION (V1's proposal against the V2 retest path and the PB-01 watchlist retest; the distinction from the code): (a) V1 (this part): computed after placement by the observer from the candidate's intended entry; it parks nothing, changes nothing and is only written to the row with `detector_version` `snr-v1` (P03-S020); zone test `_is_into_zone` with `entry_proximity_pct` (1.0 per cent, one-sided pad) and only the nearest zone; buffer `retest_sl_buffer_pct` (0.3, loader). (b) V2 (part P04; `screening/retest_monitor.py`): `RetestDiverter.maybe_divert` runs before sizing and placement and parks the signal under `wait_for_retest_enabled` (committed `false`, `config/system_config.yaml` L529; `screening/retest_monitor.py` L294); it chooses among the cached HIGH zones with `ScoredZone.contains` and `near_zone_buffer_pct` (committed 0.3, a pad on both sides; L295 to L296, L384 to L390); its stop is `band_low * (1 - sl_buffer_pct / 100)` for a long and `band_high * (1 + sl_buffer_pct / 100)` for a short, the same formula with `sl_buffer_pct` (committed 0.2; L297, L354 to L358); it writes its own `sr_detector_results` row with `would_wait_for_retest` 1 and `detector_version` `snr-v2` (L405 to L431, L285; P03-S093); its confirmation and resumption are `sr_detector/retest_confirm.py` and `continue_from_retest` (part P04). (c) PB-01 (A18; D-41, P1): the watchlist's own retest in `v3_chain/`, which reuses A08's zone functions (A08 inventory, section 2.3) and does not import `sr_detector/flags.py` (search above). INTERPRETATION: the three share the word "retest" and, for V1 and V2, the entry level (the zone's edge) and the stop formula, but not the code path, the timing (after placement against before placement), the zone test, the buffer key or the effect (a logged proposal against a parked signal). A02 K-22 records the two buffer values (section 10.2).
- P03-S078 FACT and INTERPRETATION (units in `flags.py`; A02 K-21's A08 side): `entry_proximity_pct` is divided by 100 and multiplied by the zone centre (L143) and `retest_sl_buffer_pct` is divided by 100 and applied to the band edge (L180, L185): both are percentages; `volume_surge_mult` is a multiple of the average volume (L174); `weak_breakout_frac` is a fraction of the zone's width (L164, L169); `dist_to_resistance_pct` and `dist_to_support_pct` are percentages of the entry (× 100.0, L68 to L69). INTERPRETATION: each matches the unit its committed comment or field comment states ("within this %", "this × avg", "frac×width", "this % beyond the zone"); a value entered as a fraction for one of the `_pct` keys would pass the loader and act as a hundredth of the intended pad (no range check, P03-S060).
- P03-S079 INTERPRETATION (edge inputs; from L61 to L186 and P01-S005): an `intended_entry` of 0.0 gives `None` distances but the flags are still computed (the pads use the zone centre); a NaN entry makes every comparison false, so no "into" flag, no broken zone and no breakout flag arise, while `_nearest` still returns a zone (the `min` key is NaN for every zone, so the first zone is returned, P01-S133) and the distances are NaN; a direction other than `LONG` or `BUY` (including `SELL`, empty text or a misspelling) takes the short branch (P01-S031). A HIGH zone needs two timeframes (P01-S080), so with one fetched interval no proposal can arise (P03-S056).
- P03-S080 HISTORICAL and INTERPRETATION (the docstring's flag table against the code; from P03-S014 and P03-S066 to P03-S074): `BUYING_INTO_RESISTANCE` "long entry within buffer of a HIGH resistance zone": the code tests only the nearest resistance and a one-sided pad (P03-S068); `LOW_CONFIDENCE_STRUCTURE` "nearest zone only LOW/MED confidence": the code also requires the entry to be "into" that zone (P03-S069); `WEAK_BREAKOUT` "breakout candle lacks follow-through": the code includes the poke-and-fail case and compares with the zone just below the entry (P03-S071, P03-S072); `NO_VOLUME_CONFIRMATION` "breakout without a volume surge": matches, with the zero-average exception (P03-S072 (d)); `NO_CLEAR_STRUCTURE` "no usable zones": the code raises it only when there is no zone at all, any confidence counting as usable (P03-S048), and as the only flag of a `FETCH_FAILED` row (P03-S044). The docstring's "~13% LONG win-rate problem" (L8) is a statement of the time of writing, not checked here. The differences go to the AUDIT (P03-AP04).
