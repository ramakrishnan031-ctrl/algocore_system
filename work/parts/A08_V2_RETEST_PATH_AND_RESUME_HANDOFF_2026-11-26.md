- P04-S020 FACT (`RetestResult`, L62 to L77): a `@dataclass(frozen=True)` with `state: str` and `reason: str = ""`, and three properties: `confirmed` (`state == CONFIRMED`), `rejected` (`state == REJECT`) and `terminal` (`state in (CONFIRMED, REJECT)`). - P04-S021 FACT (everything `evaluate` can return; L89 to L127): exactly seven results: `REJECT` with `"timeout"` (L90); `REJECT` with `"break_down"` (long side) or `"reclaim_up"` (short side) (L101, L108, L114); `CONFIRMED` with `"reclaim_strong_close"` (long) or `"rejection_strong_close"` (short) (L102, L109, L125); and `WAIT_BREAKOUT`, `WAIT_RETEST` or `WAIT_CONFIRM` with `"in_progress"` (L127). No other state or reason is produced.
- P04-S022 FACT (the signature, L80 to L88): keyword-only `band_low: float`, `band_high: float`, `candles: List` (comment "1m Candle-likes (.open/.high/.low/.close), chronological"), `elapsed_sec: float`, `params: RetestParams` and `direction: str = "LONG"` (comment `"LONG"/"BUY" or "SHORT"/"SELL"`). Of each candle it reads only `close`, `low` and `high` (L99, L100, L106, L107, L121, L133 to L136); `open`, `volume` and `ts` are never read.
- P04-S023 FACT and INTERPRETATION (the timeout and its clock, L89 to L90): FACT: the first statement is `if elapsed_sec > params.timeout_sec: return RetestResult(REJECT, "timeout")`, before any candle is examined. INTERPRETATION: (a) the timeout overrides every candle: a list that would confirm is rejected if the elapsed time given exceeds the timeout at the moment of the call, even if the confirming candle closed before the deadline (the function receives no candle times); (b) the test is strict, so an elapsed time equal to the timeout does not reject; (c) the function has no clock of its own: the timeout runs on whatever `elapsed_sec` the caller computes, which for the monitor is the wall-clock difference recorded in section 6; (d) a NaN `elapsed_sec` or `timeout_sec`, or a negative elapsed time, never times out (P04-S006).
- P04-S024 FACT (the levels and predicates, L92 to L109): `is_long = _is_long(direction)` (L92); `margin = params.breakout_margin_pct / 100.0` (L93); `away = params.max_away_pct / 100.0` (L94). Long side (L96 to L102): `break_level = band_high * (1.0 + margin)`, `reject_level = band_low * (1.0 - away)`, `broke = lambda c: c.close > break_level`, `failed = lambda c: c.close < reject_level`, `fail_reason = "break_down"`, `confirm_reason = "reclaim_strong_close"`. Short side, for every direction that is not long (L103 to L109): `break_level = band_low * (1.0 - margin)`, `reject_level = band_high * (1.0 + away)`, `broke = lambda c: c.close < break_level`, `failed = lambda c: c.close > reject_level`, `fail_reason = "reclaim_up"`, `confirm_reason = "rejection_strong_close"`.
- P04-S025 INTERPRETATION (the price rules in words; from P04-S024): LONG: a break is a one-minute close strictly above `band_high × (1 + m/100)`, with `m` = `breakout_margin_pct` (at the committed 0.0, any close above the band's top); the wrong-way reject is a close strictly below `band_low × (1 − a/100)`, with `a` = `max_away_pct` (at the committed 1.0, a close more than 1 % below the band's bottom). SHORT: a break is a close strictly below `band_low × (1 − m/100)`; the wrong-way reject is a close strictly above `band_high × (1 + a/100)` (at the committed 1.0, more than 1 % above the band's top). Both percentages are applied multiplicatively to the band edge they test, not to the band's width or centre (unlike `contains`, which pads by a percentage of the centre, P01-S024); both are percentages, divided by 100 (A02 K-21's A08 side, section 11). Only closes count for the break and the reject: a high or low beyond a level does not.
- P04-S026 FACT (the fold, L111 to L127): `state = WAIT_BREAKOUT` (L111); for each candle `c` in list order (L112): (1) `if failed(c): return RetestResult(REJECT, fail_reason)` (L113 to L114), in every state; (2) `if state == WAIT_BREAKOUT:` and `broke(c)`, `state = WAIT_RETEST` (L116 to L118); (3) `elif state == WAIT_RETEST:` and `c.low <= band_high and c.high >= band_low`, `state = WAIT_CONFIRM` (L119 to L122; comment L120 "retest touch: the candle's range re-enters the band (side-agnostic)."); (4) `elif state == WAIT_CONFIRM:` and `broke(c) and _strong_close(c, params.confirm_strong_close_frac, is_long)`, `return RetestResult(CONFIRMED, confirm_reason)` (L123 to L125). After the last candle: `return RetestResult(state, "in_progress")` (L127). An empty list gives `WAIT_BREAKOUT`, `"in_progress"` (unless the timeout applies).
- P04-S027 INTERPRETATION (the transitions; from P04-S026): (a) a candle makes at most one transition (an `if`/`elif` chain), so the breaking candle cannot also be the touch and the touching candle cannot also confirm: a confirmation needs at least three candles (break, touch, confirming break); (b) there is no backward transition: in `WAIT_RETEST` a candle that does not touch leaves the state as it is, and in `WAIT_CONFIRM` a candle that is not a strong break leaves it there, however far the price moves away, until the timeout or a wrong-way reject; (c) in `WAIT_BREAKOUT` a candle inside the band has no effect (the entry was inside the padded zone at the divert, section 4, so the first candles may well be); (d) the touch is the same test for both sides: the candle's range overlaps the unpadded band, both edges inclusive, with no margin or buffer; (e) the wrong-way test runs before the state step on every candle, so a close past the reject level ends the fold with `REJECT` in any state, after a break or a touch too; (f) a terminal result ends the fold and later candles are not examined. LONG and SHORT differ only in the predicates of P04-S024 and the polarity of `_strong_close`.
- P04-S028 INTERPRETATION and UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (candle order and duplicates; A05 CM-05's A08 side, P04 secondary): the fold applies the transitions in list order; nothing in the function sorts, de-duplicates or checks the times of the candles (it reads no `ts`, P04-S022), and the docstring and the parameter comment assume "chronological" order (L13, L84). A list in descending order would be read from the latest candle backwards. A duplicated candle is examined twice: three copies of one candle that closes above `band_high` with its low at or below `band_high` and a strong close (the tests' breakout candle, high 102.2, low 101.0, close 102.0 against the band 100 to 101, is one) would go break, touch and confirming break, a `CONFIRMED` from one market minute. The monitor's filter keeps the fetched order (section 6) and the fetcher returns rows in the closure's order (P02-S025, P02-S026). Whether the legacy server ever returned minute candles out of order or duplicated is UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED (A05 CM-05; P02-U03).
- P04-S029 FACT (`_strong_close`, L130 to L137): signature `_strong_close(c, frac: float, is_long: bool = True) -> bool`; docstring "Directional strong close: LONG wants the close in the UPPER frac of the 1m range, SHORT in the LOWER frac."; `rng = c.high - c.low` (L133); `if rng <= 0:` (comment "degenerate (flat) bar") `return c.close >= c.high if is_long else c.close <= c.low` (L134 to L135); otherwise `pos = (c.close - c.low) / rng if is_long else (c.high - c.close) / rng` (L136) and `return pos >= frac` (L137).
- P04-S030 INTERPRETATION (what "strong" means; from P04-S029, with the committed `confirm_strong_close_frac: 0.6`): LONG: the close must be at least 60 % of the way from the low to the high, that is, in the top 40 % of the range; SHORT: the close must be at least 60 % of the way down from the high, in the bottom 40 % of the range. A larger value is stricter; a value at or below 0 makes every bar with a positive range strong, and a value above 1 makes none strong. The comments' wording ("UPPER frac", "the strong 60%") is compared with this in section 12. Zero range (L134 to L135): a flat bar whose close equals its high and low is strong for both sides, so in `WAIT_CONFIRM` a flat one-minute bar whose close is past the break level confirms whatever the fraction is; a malformed bar with `high < low` takes the same branch. A NaN in the high, low or close gives a NaN range and position, both comparisons false, so not strong; an infinite high or low gives a position of 0 or NaN, not strong unless the fraction is at or below 0 (P04-S006).
- P04-S031 INTERPRETATION (NaN and infinite candle values in the fold; P04-S006): a NaN close neither breaks nor fails (every comparison is false), so it changes nothing; a NaN low or high makes the touch false. A positive infinite close breaks on the long side (and is not a wrong-way close) and is a wrong-way close (`reclaim_up`) on the short side; a negative infinite close is the mirror. `evaluate` validates no candle value, and `Candle.from_kite` passes NaN and infinite prices through (P01-S017).
- P04-S032 INTERPRETATION (bands and parameters outside the committed values; from P04-S024, P04-S019): `evaluate` does not check `band_low <= band_high` or that the parameters are positive. A negative `max_away_pct` moves the reject level to the other side of the band edge (for a long, above `band_low`), so closes inside the band would reject; a negative `breakout_margin_pct` moves the break level inside the band; a `confirm_strong_close_frac` outside 0 to 1 acts as in P04-S030. The loader accepts any float for these keys (P04-S019); the committed values (1.0, 0.0, 0.6) are inside the ranges the comments describe.
- P04-S033 INTERPRETATION ("pure, idempotent and restart-safe", L12 to L18): `evaluate` is a pure function of its arguments (no clock, no state, P04-S012), so the same arguments give the same result, as the tests state (`tests/unit/test_sr_v2_retest_confirm.py` L100 to L102, L169 to L171). The persisted state is not an input: the fold always starts at `WAIT_BREAKOUT` (L111). The result therefore depends only on the candle list that the caller supplies at each evaluation and on the elapsed time; "restart-safe" holds as far as the same candles since the divert can be fetched again after a restart (the monitor's window, section 6), and a different list (a fetch failure, a forming candle completed, a broker correction) can give a different result.
